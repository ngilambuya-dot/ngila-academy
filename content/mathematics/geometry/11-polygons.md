# Polygons

## 🎯 What is a Polygon?

A **polygon** is a closed 2-dimensional shape made up of **straight line segments** connected end-to-end.

### Key Properties:
- ✅ **Closed** shape (no gaps)
- ✅ **Straight sides** (no curves)
- ✅ **At least 3 sides**

### Examples of Polygons:
Triangle (3 sides) Quadrilateral (4 sides) Pentagon (5 sides)

## 📊 Types of Polygons

### By Number of Sides:

| Sides | Name | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| 3 | **Triangle** | ∆ABC |
| 4 | **Quadrilateral** | Square, rectangle |
| 5 | **Pentagon** | Regular pentagon |
| 6 | **Hexagon** | Regular hexagon |
| 7 | **Heptagon** | Regular heptagon |
| 8 | **Octagon** | Stop sign |
| 9 | **Nonagon** | - |
| 10 | **Decagon** | - |
| n | **n-gon** | General polygon |

### By Shape:

**1. Regular Polygon**
- All sides are equal
- All angles are equal

**2. Irregular Polygon**
- Sides are not all equal
- Angles are not all equal

**3. Convex Polygon**
- All interior angles < 180°
- No "indentations"

**4. Concave Polygon**
- At least one interior angle > 180°
- Has an "indentation"

## 📊 Sum of Interior Angles

### The Formula:

> **Sum of interior angles = (n - 2) × 180°**

Where n = number of sides

### Examples:

| Polygon | n | Calculation | Sum |
|---------|---|-------------|-----|
| Triangle | 3 | (3-2) × 180° | 180° |
| Quadrilateral | 4 | (4-2) × 180° | 360° |
| Pentagon | 5 | (5-2) × 180° | 540° |
| Hexagon | 6 | (6-2) × 180° | 720° |
| Octagon | 8 | (8-2) × 180° | 1080° |

### Proof:

**Given:** A polygon with n sides

**To Prove:** Sum of interior angles = (n - 2) × 180°

**Proof:**
1. From one vertex, draw diagonals to all non-adjacent vertices
2. This divides the polygon into (n - 2) triangles
3. Each triangle has angles summing to 180°
4. Sum of angles in all triangles = (n - 2) × 180°
5. This is also the sum of all interior angles

## 📊 Each Interior Angle of a Regular Polygon

### The Formula:

> **Each interior angle = [(n - 2) × 180°] / n**

### Examples:

| Polygon | n | Calculation | Each Angle |
|---------|---|-------------|------------|
| Triangle | 3 | (3-2)×180° ÷ 3 | 60° |
| Square | 4 | (4-2)×180° ÷ 4 | 90° |
| Pentagon | 5 | (5-2)×180° ÷ 5 | 108° |
| Hexagon | 6 | (6-2)×180° ÷ 6 | 120° |
| Octagon | 8 | (8-2)×180° ÷ 8 | 135° |

## 📊 Sum of Exterior Angles

### The Rule:

> **The sum of the exterior angles of any polygon is 360°.**
Exterior Angle = 180° - Interior Angle

### Each Exterior Angle of a Regular Polygon:

> **Each exterior angle = 360° / n**

### Examples:

| Polygon | n | Each Exterior Angle |
|---------|---|---------------------|
| Triangle | 3 | 120° |
| Square | 4 | 90° |
| Pentagon | 5 | 72° |
| Hexagon | 6 | 60° |
| Octagon | 8 | 45° |

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Sum of Interior Angles

**Problem:** Find the sum of interior angles of a hexagon.

**Solution:**
n = 6
Sum = (6 - 2) × 180°
Sum = 4 × 180° = 720°

text

### Example 2: Each Interior Angle

**Problem:** Find each interior angle of a regular pentagon.

**Solution:**
n = 5
Each interior = [(5 - 2) × 180°] / 5
Each interior = (3 × 180°) / 5
Each interior = 540° / 5 = 108°

text

### Example 3: Finding Number of Sides

**Problem:** A regular polygon has each interior angle 150°. How many sides?

**Solution:**
150° = [(n - 2) × 180°] / n
150n = 180n - 360
360 = 30n
n = 12 (dodecagon)

text

### Example 4: Exterior Angles

**Problem:** A regular polygon has each exterior angle 30°. How many sides?

**Solution:**
30° = 360° / n
n = 360° / 30° = 12

text

### Example 5: Irregular Polygon

**Problem:** In a pentagon, four angles are 100°, 110°, 120°, 130°. Find the fifth.

**Solution:**
Sum of interior angles = (5 - 2) × 180° = 540°
Sum of known angles = 100° + 110° + 120° + 130° = 460°
Fifth angle = 540° - 460° = 80°

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Tiling

> Regular hexagons, squares, and triangles can tile a floor perfectly.
>
> **Why:** Their angles divide 360° evenly.

### Application 2: Architecture

> Buildings often use regular polygons for design.
>
> **Examples:** Pentagon building, octagonal rooms.

### Application 3: Road Signs

> Stop signs are octagons.
>
> **Why:** Easy to recognize.

**Question 3:** A regular polygon has each interior angle 120°. How many sides?
