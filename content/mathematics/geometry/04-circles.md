Circles
🎯 What is a Circle?
A circle is a set of points that are all the same distance from a fixed point called the centre.

text
       ____
     /      \
    |   ●    |  ← Centre
     \      /
      ‾‾‾‾‾
📊 Parts of a Circle
text
       ____
     /      \
    |  r  ●  |  ← r = radius
     \      /
      ‾‾‾‾‾
Part	Definition	Symbol
Centre	Fixed point in the middle	O
Radius	Distance from centre to edge	r
Diameter	Distance across circle through centre	d = 2r
Circumference	Distance around the circle	C
Chord	Line segment connecting two points on circle	-
Arc	Part of the circumference	-
Sector	Region bounded by two radii and an arc	-
Segment	Region bounded by a chord and an arc	-
Tangent	Line touching circle at exactly one point	-
Secant	Line intersecting circle at two points	-
🔢 Circumference
Formula:

text
C = 2πr
or

text
C = πd
Where π ≈ 3.14159 (or 22/7)

Example 1: Finding Circumference
Problem: Find the circumference of a circle with radius 7 cm.

Solution:

text
C = 2πr
C = 2 × 22/7 × 7
C = 44 cm
Example 2: Finding Circumference from Diameter
Problem: Find the circumference of a circle with diameter 10 cm.

Solution:

text
C = πd
C = 3.14 × 10
C = 31.4 cm
🔢 Area of a Circle
Formula:

text
A = πr²
Example 3: Finding Area
Problem: Find the area of a circle with radius 7 cm.

Solution:

text
A = πr²
A = 22/7 × 7²
A = 22/7 × 49
A = 154 cm²
Example 4: Finding Radius from Area
Problem: A circle has area 154 cm². Find the radius.

Solution:

text
A = πr²
154 = 22/7 × r²
r² = 154 × 7/22
r² = 49
r = 7 cm
🔢 Arc Length
An arc is part of the circumference.

Formula:

text
Arc Length = (θ/360°) × 2πr
Where θ is the angle at the centre in degrees

Example 5: Finding Arc Length
Problem: Find the length of an arc with radius 10 cm and angle 60°.

Solution:

text
Arc Length = (60/360) × 2π × 10
Arc Length = (1/6) × 20π
Arc Length = 10.47 cm
🔢 Sector Area
A sector is a "slice" of a circle.

Formula:

text
Sector Area = (θ/360°) × πr²
Example 6: Finding Sector Area
Problem: Find the area of a sector with radius 10 cm and angle 60°.

Solution:

text
Sector Area = (60/360) × π × 10²
Sector Area = (1/6) × 100π
Sector Area = 52.36 cm²
🔢 Segment Area
A segment is the region between a chord and an arc.

Formula:

text
Segment Area = Sector Area - Triangle Area
Example 7: Finding Segment Area
Problem: Find the area of a segment with radius 10 cm and angle 60°.

Solution:

text
Sector Area = 52.36 cm²
Triangle Area = ½ × 10 × 10 × sin(60°) = 43.30 cm²
Segment Area = 52.36 - 43.30 = 9.06 cm²
🌍 Real-Life Applications
Application 1: Running Track
Problem: A running track has a semicircular ends. Find the total length.

Solution:

text
Straight sections: 2 × 100 m = 200 m
Curved sections: 2 × (½ × 2πr) = 2πr
If r = 30 m: 2π × 30 = 188.5 m
Total = 200 + 188.5 = 388.5 m
Application 2: Pizza
Problem: A pizza has diameter 30 cm. What is the area?

Solution:

text
r = 15 cm
A = π × 15² = 706.86 cm²
Application 3: Circular Garden
Problem: A circular garden has radius 5 m. A path 1 m wide is built around it. Find the area of the path.

Solution:

text
Outer radius = 5 + 1 = 6 m
Garden area = π × 5² = 78.54 m²
Total area = π × 6² = 113.10 m²
Path area = 113.10 - 78.54 = 34.56 m²
