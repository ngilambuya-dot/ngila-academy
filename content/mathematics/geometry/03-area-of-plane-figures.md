# Area of Plane Figures

## 🎯 What is Area?

**Area** is the amount of space inside a 2-dimensional shape. It is measured in square units.

### Key Concept:
Area = Number of square units inside a shape

text

### Common Units:
- mm²
- cm²
- m²
- km²
- hectares
- acres

---

## 📊 Area of a Square

**Formula:**
A = s²

text

**Example:** Side = 5 cm
A = 5² = 25 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Area of a Rectangle

**Formula:**
A = l × w

text

**Example:** Length = 8 cm, Width = 5 cm
A = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Area of a Triangle

**Formula:**
A = ½ × b × h

text

**Example:** Base = 6 cm, Height = 4 cm
A = ½ × 6 × 4 = 12 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Area of a Parallelogram

**Formula:**
A = b × h

text

**Example:** Base = 8 cm, Height = 5 cm
A = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Area of a Trapezium

**Formula:**
A = ½(a + b) × h

text

**Example:** a = 5 cm, b = 9 cm, h = 4 cm
A = ½(5 + 9) × 4 = 28 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Area of a Rhombus

**Formula:**
A = ½ × d₁ × d₂

text
Where d₁ and d₂ are the diagonals

**Example:** d₁ = 8 cm, d₂ = 6 cm
A = ½ × 8 × 6 = 24 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Area of a Circle

**Formula:**
A = πr²

text
Where r = radius, π ≈ 3.14159

**Example:** r = 7 cm
A = 3.14159 × 7² = 153.94 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Composite Areas

### Example 1: Combined Shapes

**Problem:** Find the area of this shape (a rectangle with a triangle on top).

**Solution:**
Rectangle: l = 8 cm, w = 4 cm
Area = 8 × 4 = 32 cm²

Triangle: b = 8 cm, h = 3 cm
Area = ½ × 8 × 3 = 12 cm²

Total area = 32 + 12 = 44 cm²

text

### Example 2: Shaded Region

**Problem:** Find the area of the shaded region (circle inside a square).

**Solution:**
Square: s = 10 cm
Area = 10² = 100 cm²

Circle: r = 5 cm
Area = π × 5² = 78.54 cm²

Shaded area = 100 - 78.54 = 21.46 cm²

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Painting a Wall

**Problem:** A rectangular wall is 4 m by 3 m. A 1 m by 1 m window is removed. What area needs painting?

**Solution:**
Wall area = 4 × 3 = 12 m²
Window area = 1 × 1 = 1 m²
Paint area = 12 - 1 = 11 m²

text

### Application 2: Carpeting a Room

**Problem:** A room is 5 m by 4 m. How much carpet is needed?

**Solution:**
Area = 5 × 4 = 20 m²

text

### Application 3: Tiling

**Problem:** A kitchen floor is 3 m by 2.5 m. Tiles are 50 cm by 50 cm. How many tiles are needed?

**Solution:**
Floor area = 3 × 2.5 = 7.5 m²
Tile area = 0.5 × 0.5 = 0.25 m²
Number of tiles = 7.5 ÷ 0.25 = 30 tiles

text
