# Introduction to Measurement

## 🎯 What is Measurement?

**Measurement** is the process of finding the size, length, amount, or degree of something using standard units.

### Why Measurement Matters:
- **Construction:** Building houses, roads, bridges
- **Cooking:** Following recipes accurately
- **Medicine:** Giving correct doses
- **Science:** Conducting experiments
- **Commerce:** Buying and selling goods
- **Daily Life:** Time, distance, weight

---

## 📊 Physical Quantities and Units

| Quantity | SI Unit | Symbol | Common Units |
|----------|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Length** | Metre | m | mm, cm, km, inch, foot, yard, mile |
| **Mass** | Kilogram | kg | g, mg, tonne, pound, ounce |
| **Time** | Second | s | min, hour, day, week, month, year |
| **Temperature** | Kelvin | K | °C, °F |
| **Capacity** | Litre | L | mL, kL, gallon, pint |
| **Area** | Square metre | m² | cm², km², hectare |
| **Volume** | Cubic metre | m³ | cm³, L, mL |

## 📏 Measuring Length

### Units of Length:

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent |
|------|--------|------------|
| Millimetre | mm | 1/1000 m |
| Centimetre | cm | 1/100 m |
| Metre | m | 1 m |
| Kilometre | km | 1000 m |

### Conversions:
1 km = 1000 m
1 m = 100 cm
1 cm = 10 mm
1 m = 1000 mm

## 🌡️ Measuring Temperature

### Celsius and Fahrenheit:

| Scale | Freezing Point | Boiling Point |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| Celsius (°C) | 0°C | 100°C |
| Fahrenheit (°F) | 32°F | 212°F |

### Conversion Formulas:
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
°C = (°F - 32) × 5/9

text

### Example 11: Temperature Conversion

**Problem:** Convert 25°C to Fahrenheit

**Solution:**
°F = (25 × 9/5) + 32
°F = 45 + 32 = 77°F

text

---

### Example 12: Temperature Conversion

**Problem:** Convert 68°F to Celsius

**Solution:**
°C = (68 - 32) × 5/9
°C = 36 × 5/9 = 20°C

text
