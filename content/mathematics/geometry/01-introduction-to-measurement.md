# Introduction to Measurement

## 🎯 What is Measurement?

**Measurement** is the process of finding the size, length, amount, or degree of something using standard units.

---

## 📊 Physical Quantities and Units

| Quantity | SI Unit | Symbol | Common Units |
|----------|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Length** | Metre | m | mm, cm, km |
| **Mass** | Kilogram | kg | g, mg, tonne |
| **Time** | Second | s | min, hour, day |
| **Temperature** | Kelvin | K | °C, °F |
| **Capacity** | Litre | L | mL, kL |

---

## 📏 Measuring Length

### Units of Length:

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent |
|------|--------|------------|
| Millimetre | mm | 1/1000 m |
| Centimetre | cm | 1/100 m |
| Metre | m | 1 m |
| Kilometre | km | 1000 m |

### Conversions:
1 km = 1000 m
1 m = 100 cm
1 cm = 10 mm

# Perimeter

## 🎯 What is Perimeter?

**Perimeter** is the total distance around the outside of a shape.

### Key Concept:
Perimeter = Sum of all sides

text

---

## 📊 Perimeter Formulas

| Shape | Formula |
|-------|---------|
| Square | P = 4s |
| Rectangle | P = 2(l + w) |
| Triangle | P = a + b + c |

### Square
P = 4s

text
Example: Side = 5 cm → P = 20 cm

### Rectangle
P = 2(l + w)

text
Example: l = 8 cm, w = 5 cm → P = 26 cm

# Area of Plane Figures

## 🎯 What is Area?

**Area** is the amount of space inside a 2-dimensional shape.

### Key Concept:
Area = Number of square units inside a shape

text

---

## 📊 Area Formulas

| Shape | Formula |
|-------|---------|
| Square | A = s² |
| Rectangle | A = l × w |
| Triangle | A = ½ × b × h |
| Circle | A = πr² |

### Square
A = s²

text
Example: Side = 5 cm → A = 25 cm²

### Rectangle
A = l × w

text
Example: l = 8 cm, w = 5 cm → A = 40 cm²
