# Perimeter

## 🎯 What is Perimeter?

**Perimeter** is the total distance around the outside of a shape. It is the sum of all the sides of a shape.

### Key Concept:
Perimeter = Sum of all sides

## 📊 Perimeter of Common Shapes

### Square
A square has four equal sides.
s
┌──────┐
│ │ s
└──────┘

text

**Formula:**
P = 4s

text
Where s = length of one side

**Example:** Side = 5 cm
P = 4 × 5 = 20 cm

text

---

### Rectangle
A rectangle has two pairs of equal sides.
l
┌─────────┐
│ │ w
└─────────┘

text

**Formula:**
P = 2(l + w)

text
Where l = length, w = width

**Example:** Length = 8 cm, Width = 5 cm
P = 2(8 + 5) = 2(13) = 26 cm

text

---

### Triangle
A triangle has three sides.
/
/
/
/______\

text

**Formula:**
P = a + b + c

text
Where a, b, c are the three sides

**Example:** Sides = 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm
P = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 cm

text

---

### Parallelogram
A parallelogram has two pairs of equal sides.

**Formula:**
P = 2(a + b)

text
Where a and b are adjacent sides

**Example:** Sides = 6 cm and 4 cm
P = 2(6 + 4) = 2(10) = 20 cm

text

---

### Rhombus
A rhombus has four equal sides.

**Formula:**
P = 4s

text
Where s = side length

**Example:** Side = 7 cm
P = 4 × 7 = 28 cm

text

---

### Trapezium (Trapezoid)
A trapezium has one pair of parallel sides.

**Formula:**
P = a + b + c + d

text
Where a, b, c, d are all four sides

## 🔢 Finding Missing Dimensions

### Example 1: Finding Missing Side

**Problem:** A rectangle has perimeter 30 cm and length 10 cm. Find the width.

**Solution:**
P = 2(l + w)
30 = 2(10 + w)
30 = 20 + 2w
10 = 2w
w = 5 cm

text

---

### Example 2: Finding Missing Side of a Square

**Problem:** A square has perimeter 32 cm. Find the side length.

**Solution:**
P = 4s
32 = 4s
s = 8 cm

text

---

### Example 3: Finding Missing Side of a Triangle

**Problem:** A triangle has perimeter 25 cm. Two sides are 8 cm and 10 cm. Find the third side.

**Solution:**
P = a + b + c
25 = 8 + 10 + c
25 = 18 + c
c = 7 cm

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Fencing a Garden

**Problem:** A rectangular garden is 12 m by 8 m. How much fencing is needed to enclose it?

**Solution:**
P = 2(12 + 8) = 2(20) = 40 m

text

---

### Application 2: Picture Frame

**Problem:** A square photo frame has side 25 cm. What length of wood is needed?

**Solution:**
P = 4 × 25 = 100 cm

text

---

### Application 3: Running Track

**Problem:** A rectangular field 100 m by 50 m has a 2 m wide path around it. Find the perimeter of the outer edge.

**Solution:**
Outer length = 100 + 2 + 2 = 104 m
Outer width = 50 + 2 + 2 = 54 m
P = 2(104 + 54) = 2(158) = 316 m

text
