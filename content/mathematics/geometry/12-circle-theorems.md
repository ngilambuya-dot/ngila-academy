# Circle Theorems

## 🎯 What are Circle Theorems?

**Circle theorems** are rules that describe the relationships between angles, lines, and points in a circle. They are among the most important tools in geometry.

### Key Concepts:
- ✅ **Angle at centre** vs **angle at circumference**
- ✅ **Same segment** theorems
- ✅ **Cyclic quadrilateral** properties
- ✅ **Tangent** properties

---

## 📊 Theorem 1: Angle at Centre

### The Rule:

> **The angle at the centre of a circle is twice the angle at the circumference, standing on the same arc.**

**Formula:**
∠AOB = 2 × ∠ACB

**Example:** If ∠ACB = 40°, then ∠AOB = 80°.

## 📊 Theorem 8: Chord Properties

### The Rule:

> **The perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord bisects the chord.**
● (Centre)
/
/
/
A───●───B
/
/
C─────D

text

**Formula:**
OC ⟂ AB, and C is the midpoint of AB

text

**Example:** If AB = 10 cm, then AC = CB = 5 cm.

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Angle at Centre

**Problem:** ∠ACB = 35°. Find ∠AOB.

**Solution:**
∠AOB = 2 × ∠ACB (Angle at centre is twice angle at circumference)
∠AOB = 2 × 35° = 70°

text

### Example 2: Angle in a Semicircle

**Problem:** AB is a diameter. ∠ACB = ?

**Solution:**
∠ACB = 90° (Angle in a semicircle is 90°)

text

### Example 3: Cyclic Quadrilateral

**Problem:** In a cyclic quadrilateral, ∠A = 110°. Find ∠C.

**Solution:**
∠A + ∠C = 180° (Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180°)
110° + ∠C = 180°
∠C = 70°

text

### Example 4: Tangent-Radius

**Problem:** Find ∠OTP if OT is radius and TP is tangent.

**Solution:**
∠OTP = 90° (Radius perpendicular to tangent at point of contact)

text

### Example 5: Alternate Segment

**Problem:** ∠CTB = 40° (where CT is tangent). Find ∠CAB.

**Solution:**
∠CAB = ∠CTB = 40° (Alternate segment theorem)

text
