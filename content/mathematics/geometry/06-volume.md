# Volume

## 🎯 What is Volume?

**Volume** is the amount of space occupied by a 3-dimensional object. It is measured in cubic units.

### Key Concept:
Volume = Number of cubic units inside a shape

text

### Common Units:
- mm³
- cm³
- m³
- L (litres) = 1000 cm³
- mL = 1 cm³

---

## 📊 Volume of a Cube

A cube has all sides equal.

**Formula:**
V = s³

text

**Example:** Side = 5 cm
V = 5³ = 125 cm³

text

---

## 📊 Volume of a Cuboid

A cuboid has length, width, and height.

**Formula:**
V = l × w × h

text

**Example:** l = 8 cm, w = 5 cm, h = 3 cm
V = 8 × 5 × 3 = 120 cm³

text

---

## 📊 Volume of a Cylinder

A cylinder has circular bases.

**Formula:**
V = πr²h

text

**Example:** r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm
V = π × 7² × 10
V = 22/7 × 49 × 10 = 1540 cm³

text

---

## 📊 Volume of a Cone

A cone has a circular base and a pointed top.

**Formula:**
V = ⅓πr²h

text

**Example:** r = 7 cm, h = 12 cm
V = ⅓ × π × 7² × 12
V = ⅓ × 22/7 × 49 × 12 = 616 cm³

text

---

## 📊 Volume of a Sphere

A sphere has no edges or vertices.

**Formula:**
V = ⁴⁄₃πr³

text

**Example:** r = 7 cm
V = ⁴⁄₃ × 22/7 × 7³
V = ⁴⁄₃ × 22/7 × 343 = 1437.33 cm³

text

---

## 📊 Volume of a Pyramid

A pyramid has a base and triangular faces meeting at a point.

**Formula:**
V = ⅓ × Base Area × Height

text

**Example:** Square base = 6 cm, height = 10 cm
Base Area = 6 × 6 = 36 cm²
V = ⅓ × 36 × 10 = 120 cm³

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Water Tank

**Problem:** A cylindrical water tank has radius 2 m and height 3 m. How much water can it hold?

**Solution:**
V = π × 2² × 3 = 12π = 37.70 m³
1 m³ = 1000 L
Capacity = 37.70 × 1000 = 37,700 L

text

### Application 2: Packaging

**Problem:** A cuboid box is 30 cm × 20 cm × 15 cm. How many 5 cm cubes can fit?

**Solution:**
Box volume = 30 × 20 × 15 = 9000 cm³
Cube volume = 5³ = 125 cm³
Number = 9000 ÷ 125 = 72 cubes

text

### Application 3: Ice Cream

**Problem:** A cone has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. A scoop of ice cream (sphere) of radius 3 cm is placed on top. Total volume?

**Solution:**
Cone V = ⅓ × π × 3² × 10 = 94.25 cm³
Sphere V = ⁴⁄₃ × π × 3³ = 113.10 cm³
Total = 94.25 + 113.10 = 207.35 cm³

text
