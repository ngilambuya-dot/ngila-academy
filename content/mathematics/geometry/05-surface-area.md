# Surface Area

## 🎯 What is Surface Area?

**Surface area** is the total area of all the faces or surfaces of a 3-dimensional shape.

### Key Concept:
Surface Area = Sum of areas of all faces

text

---

## 📊 Surface Area of a Cube

A cube has 6 identical square faces.

**Formula:**
SA = 6s²

text

**Example:** Side = 5 cm
SA = 6 × 5² = 6 × 25 = 150 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Surface Area of a Cuboid

A cuboid has 3 pairs of rectangular faces.

**Formula:**
SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)

text
Where l = length, w = width, h = height

**Example:** l = 8 cm, w = 5 cm, h = 3 cm
SA = 2(8×5 + 8×3 + 5×3)
SA = 2(40 + 24 + 15)
SA = 2(79) = 158 cm²

text

**Lateral Surface Area (LSA)** = Area of side faces only
LSA = 2h(l + w)

text
LSA = 2(3)(8 + 5) = 78 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Surface Area of a Cylinder

A cylinder has 2 circular bases and 1 curved surface.

**Curved Surface Area (CSA):**
CSA = 2πrh

text

**Total Surface Area (TSA):**
TSA = 2πr² + 2πrh = 2πr(r + h)

text

**Example:** r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm
CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm²
TSA = 2 × 22/7 × 7² + 440 = 308 + 440 = 748 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Surface Area of a Cone

A cone has 1 circular base and 1 curved surface.

**Curved Surface Area (CSA):**
CSA = πrl

text
Where l = slant height

**Total Surface Area (TSA):**
TSA = πr² + πrl = πr(r + l)

text

**Example:** r = 7 cm, l = 10 cm
CSA = 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 220 cm²
TSA = 22/7 × 7² + 220 = 154 + 220 = 374 cm²

text

---

## 📊 Surface Area of a Sphere

A sphere has 1 curved surface.

**Formula:**
SA = 4πr²

text

**Example:** r = 7 cm
SA = 4 × 22/7 × 7² = 616 cm²

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Painting a Room

**Problem:** A room is 4 m by 3 m by 2.5 m. What is the total surface area of walls and ceiling to paint?

**Solution:**
Walls: 2(4×2.5 + 3×2.5) = 2(10 + 7.5) = 35 m²
Ceiling: 4 × 3 = 12 m²
Total = 35 + 12 = 47 m²

text

### Application 2: Packaging

**Problem:** A cylindrical tin has radius 5 cm and height 15 cm. What is the area of metal needed?

**Solution:**
TSA = 2πr(r + h)
TSA = 2π × 5(5 + 15) = 10π × 20 = 628.32 cm²

text

### Application 3: Sphere

**Problem:** A ball has radius 10 cm. How much leather is needed to cover it?

**Solution:**
SA = 4π × 10² = 1256.64 cm²
