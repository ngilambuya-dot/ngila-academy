# Congruency and Similarity

## 🎯 What is Congruency?

**Congruent shapes** are shapes that are **identical** in shape and size. One can be transformed into the other by rotation, reflection, or translation.

### Key Concept:
> **Congruent shapes are exactly the same—same shape, same size.**

### Symbol:
∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF

text
(Read as "Triangle ABC is congruent to triangle DEF")

### Properties:
- ✅ **Corresponding sides** are equal
- ✅ **Corresponding angles** are equal
- ✅ **Area** is equal
- ✅ **Perimeter** is equal

---

## 📊 What is Similarity?

**Similar shapes** have the **same shape** but **different sizes**. One is an enlargement of the other.

### Key Concept:
> **Similar shapes have the same shape but different sizes.**

### Symbol:
∆ABC ∼ ∆DEF

text
(Read as "Triangle ABC is similar to triangle DEF")

### Properties:
- ✅ **Corresponding angles** are equal
- ✅ **Corresponding sides** are in proportion
- ✅ **Scale factor** relates the sizes
- ✅ **Area ratio** = (scale factor)²
- ✅ **Volume ratio** = (scale factor)³

## 📊 Similarity Tests for Triangles

### 1. AA (Angle-Angle)

> **If two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of another triangle, the triangles are similar.**

/ \ /
/\ /

∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠E

text

> **If the three sides of one triangle are proportional to the three sides of another triangle, the triangles are similar.**

> **If two sides of one triangle are proportional to two sides of another triangle and the included angles are equal, the triangles are similar.**

## 📊 Scale Factor

**Scale factor** is the ratio of corresponding sides in similar shapes.

### Formula:
Scale Factor = Length of image / Length of original

text

### Effects:

| Scale Factor | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| > 1 | Enlargement |
| = 1 | Same size (congruent) |
| < 1 | Reduction |
| Negative | Enlargement in opposite direction |

### Area and Volume:
Area Ratio = (Scale Factor)²
Volume Ratio = (Scale Factor)³

text

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Congruent Triangles (SSS)

**Problem:** Triangle ABC has sides 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm. Triangle DEF has sides 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm. Are they congruent?

**Solution:**
AB = DE = 3 cm
BC = EF = 4 cm
CA = FD = 5 cm

All three sides are equal. By SSS, ∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF.

text

---

### Example 2: Congruent Triangles (SAS)

**Problem:** In ∆ABC, AB = 5 cm, AC = 6 cm, ∠A = 60°. In ∆DEF, DE = 5 cm, DF = 6 cm, ∠D = 60°. Are they congruent?

**Solution:**
AB = DE = 5 cm
∠A = ∠D = 60°
AC = DF = 6 cm

Two sides and included angle are equal. By SAS, ∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF.

text

---

### Example 3: Similar Triangles

**Problem:** In ∆ABC, ∠A = 50°, ∠B = 60°. In ∆DEF, ∠D = 50°, ∠E = 60°. Are they similar?

**Solution:**
∠A = ∠D = 50°
∠B = ∠E = 60°

Two angles are equal. By AA, ∆ABC ∼ ∆DEF.

text

---

### Example 4: Finding Unknown Side

**Problem:** Two triangles are similar. ∆ABC has sides 3, 4, 5. ∆DEF has side DE = 6. Find EF and FD if scale factor is 2.

**Solution:**
Scale factor = 2
EF = BC × 2 = 4 × 2 = 8
FD = CA × 2 = 5 × 2 = 10

text

---

### Example 5: Area Ratio

**Problem:** Two similar triangles have scale factor 3. What is the ratio of their areas?

**Solution:**
Area Ratio = (Scale Factor)²
Area Ratio = 3² = 9
So the larger triangle has 9 times the area.

text

---

### Example 6: Volume Ratio

**Problem:** Two similar cubes have scale factor 2. What is the ratio of their volumes?

**Solution:**
Volume Ratio = (Scale Factor)³
Volume Ratio = 2³ = 8
So the larger cube has 8 times the volume.

text

## 📊 Summary

### Congruence Tests:

| Test | Conditions |
|------|------------|
| SSS | All three sides equal |
| SAS | Two sides and included angle equal |
| ASA | Two angles and included side equal |
| RHS | Hypotenuse and one side equal |

### Similarity Tests:

| Test | Conditions |
|------|------------|
| AA | Two angles equal |
| SSS | All sides proportional |
| SAS | Two sides proportional and included angle equal |

### Scale Factor Effects:

| Quantity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Side | Scale Factor |
| Area | (Scale Factor)² |
| Volume | (Scale Factor)³ |
