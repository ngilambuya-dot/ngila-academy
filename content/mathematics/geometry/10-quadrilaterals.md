# Quadrilaterals

## 🎯 What is a Quadrilateral?

A **quadrilateral** is a polygon with **four sides**, **four vertices**, and **four angles**.

### Key Properties:
- ✅ It has **4 sides**
- ✅ It has **4 angles**
- ✅ The sum of the interior angles is **360°**

## 📊 Angle Sum of a Quadrilateral

### The Rule:

> **The sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral is 360°.**
∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360°

**Proof:**
1. Diagonal AC divides quadrilateral into two triangles: ∆ABC and ∆ADC
2. ∠A₁ + ∠B + ∠C₁ = 180° (Angle sum of ∆ABC)
3. ∠A₂ + ∠D + ∠C₂ = 180° (Angle sum of ∆ADC)
4. (∠A₁ + ∠A₂) + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360°
5. ∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360°

## 📊 Properties of Parallelograms

### The Rules:

> **In a parallelogram:**

1. ✅ Opposite sides are parallel and equal
2. ✅ Opposite angles are equal
3. ✅ Diagonals bisect each other
4. ✅ Adjacent angles are supplementary

---

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Finding Missing Angles

**Problem:** In a parallelogram, ∠A = 70°. Find the other angles.

**Solution:**
∠A = ∠C = 70° (Opposite angles are equal)
∠A + ∠B = 180° (Adjacent angles are supplementary)
70° + ∠B = 180°
∠B = 110°
∠B = ∠D = 110° (Opposite angles are equal)

text

### Example 2: Finding Missing Angles in a Rectangle

**Problem:** In a rectangle, ∠A = 90°. Find all angles.

**Solution:**
∠A = ∠B = ∠C = ∠D = 90° (All angles in a rectangle are right angles)

text

### Example 3: Square Properties

**Problem:** In a square, side = 5 cm. Find the perimeter and area.

**Solution:**
Perimeter = 4 × 5 = 20 cm
Area = 5² = 25 cm²

## 📊 Summary

### Quadrilateral Types and Properties:

| Shape | Sides | Angles | Diagonals |
|-------|-------|--------|-----------|
| Square | All equal | All 90° | Equal, perpendicular |
| Rectangle | Opposite equal | All 90° | Equal |
| Parallelogram | Opposite equal | Opposite equal | Bisect each other |
| Rhombus | All equal | Opposite equal | Perpendicular |
| Trapezium | One pair parallel | Varies | Varies |
| Kite | Two pairs adjacent | One pair equal | Perpendicular |

### Angle Sum:
Sum of interior angles = 360°
