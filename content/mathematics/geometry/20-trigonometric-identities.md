# Trigonometric Identities and Proofs

## 🎯 What are Trigonometric Identities?

**Trigonometric identities** are equations that are true for all values of the variable. They are essential for simplifying expressions and solving equations.

### Key Concept:
> **Identities are always true, regardless of the angle.**

---

## 📊 Fundamental Identities

### 1. Ratio Identity
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
cot θ = cos θ / sin θ

text

### 2. Pythagorean Identities
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
1 + cot²θ = csc²θ

text

### 3. Reciprocal Identities
csc θ = 1 / sin θ
sec θ = 1 / cos θ
cot θ = 1 / tan θ

text

---

## 📊 Proof of Pythagorean Identity

### Statement:
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

text

### Proof:

From a right-angled triangle:
sin θ = O/H
cos θ = A/H

text

Therefore:
sin²θ + cos²θ = (O/H)² + (A/H)²
sin²θ + cos²θ = O²/H² + A²/H²
sin²θ + cos²θ = (O² + A²)/H²

text

From Pythagoras:
O² + A² = H²

text

Therefore:
sin²θ + cos²θ = H²/H² = 1

text

Hence:
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

text

---

## 📊 Proof of tan θ Identity

### Statement:
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

text

### Proof:

From a right-angled triangle:
sin θ = O/H
cos θ = A/H

text

Therefore:
sin θ / cos θ = (O/H) / (A/H)
sin θ / cos θ = O/H × H/A
sin θ / cos θ = O/A

text

But:
tan θ = O/A

text

Therefore:
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

text

## 📊 Compound Angle Formulas

### Sine of a Sum:
sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B

text

### Sine of a Difference:
sin(A - B) = sin A cos B - cos A sin B

text

### Cosine of a Sum:
cos(A + B) = cos A cos B - sin A sin B

text

### Cosine of a Difference:
cos(A - B) = cos A cos B + sin A sin B

text

### Tangent of a Sum:
tan(A + B) = (tan A + tan B) / (1 - tan A tan B)

text

### Tangent of a Difference:
tan(A - B) = (tan A - tan B) / (1 + tan A tan B)

text

## 📊 Double Angle Formulas
sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ
cos 2θ = cos²θ - sin²θ = 2cos²θ - 1 = 1 - 2sin²θ
tan 2θ = (2 tan θ) / (1 - tan²θ)

text

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Proving an Identity

**Problem:** Prove that sin θ cot θ = cos θ

**Solution:**
LHS = sin θ cot θ
= sin θ × (cos θ / sin θ)
= cos θ
= RHS

text

### Example 2: Proving an Identity

**Problem:** Prove that tan²θ + 1 = sec²θ

**Solution:**
LHS = tan²θ + 1
= (sin²θ / cos²θ) + 1
= (sin²θ + cos²θ) / cos²θ
= 1 / cos²θ
= sec²θ
= RHS

text

### Example 3: Proving an Identity

**Problem:** Prove that (sin θ + cos θ)² = 1 + 2 sin θ cos θ

**Solution:**
LHS = (sin θ + cos θ)²
= sin²θ + 2 sin θ cos θ + cos²θ
= (sin²θ + cos²θ) + 2 sin θ cos θ
= 1 + 2 sin θ cos θ
= RHS

text

### Example 4: Using Double Angle

**Problem:** Find sin 60° using sin 2θ

**Solution:**
sin 60° = sin(2 × 30°)
= 2 sin 30° cos 30°
= 2 × (1/2) × (√3/2)
= √3/2

text
