Angles
🎯 What is an Angle?
An angle is formed by two rays sharing a common endpoint called the vertex.

text
        B
        \ 
         \
          \
    A─────●─────C
         Vertex
Parts of an Angle:
Vertex: The common endpoint (point B)

Arms: The two rays (BA and BC)

Interior: The inside of the angle

Exterior: The outside of the angle

📊 Types of Angles
1. Zero Angle
Exactly 0° (both rays overlap)

2. Acute Angle
Between 0° and 90°

text
     /\
    /  \
   /    \
  /______\
3. Right Angle
Exactly 90°

text
    │
    │
    │
    └─────
4. Obtuse Angle
Between 90° and 180°

text
     /\
    /  \
   /    \
  /      \
 /________\
5. Straight Angle
Exactly 180° (a straight line)

text
──────────────
6. Reflex Angle
Between 180° and 360°

7. Complete Angle
Exactly 360° (full rotation)

text
    ●
   / \
  /   \
 /     \
/       \
📊 Measuring Angles with a Protractor
Steps:
Place the centre of the protractor on the vertex

Align the baseline with one arm

Read the angle where the other arm crosses

📊 Drawing Angles with a Protractor
Steps:
Draw a baseline

Place the centre of the protractor on the endpoint

Mark the desired angle

Draw the second arm through the mark

📊 Angle Relationships
Complementary Angles
Two angles that sum to 90°

text
∠A + ∠B = 90°
Example: 30° and 60° are complementary

Supplementary Angles
Two angles that sum to 180°

text
∠A + ∠B = 180°
Example: 110° and 70° are supplementary

Vertically Opposite Angles
Angles formed by two intersecting lines are equal.

text
     a
     │
  ───┼───
     │ b
text
a = b (vertically opposite)
📊 Angles on a Straight Line
Angles on a straight line sum to 180°.

text
∠A + ∠B = 180°
📊 Angles Around a Point
Angles around a point sum to 360°.

text
∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360°
🌍 Real-Life Applications
Application 1: Clock Angles
Problem: What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:00?

Solution:

text
Each hour = 30° (360° ÷ 12)
At 3:00, hour hand at 3, minute hand at 12
Angle = 3 × 30° = 90°
Application 2: Navigation
Problem: A ship turns 45° to the right. What is the new direction?

Solution:

text
If heading North (0°), turn 45° right = 45°
