# Introduction to Trigonometry (SOH CAH TOA)

## 🎯 What is Trigonometry?

**Trigonometry** is the study of the relationships between the sides and angles of triangles. The word comes from Greek: *trigonon* (triangle) and *metron* (measure).

### Key Concept:
> **Trigonometry helps us find unknown sides and angles in right-angled triangles.**

---

## 📊 The Right-Angled Triangle

In a right-angled triangle, we have three important sides relative to an angle:
A
|
|
Opposite | \ Hypotenuse
|
|____
B C
Adjacent

text

### Definitions:
- **Hypotenuse:** The longest side, opposite the right angle (90°)
- **Opposite:** The side opposite the angle we're looking at (θ)
- **Adjacent:** The side next to the angle we're looking at (θ)

---

## 📊 The Three Trigonometric Ratios

### SOH CAH TOA (The Memory Aid)

> **SOH** = **S**ine = **O**pposite / **H**ypotenuse
> **CAH** = **C**osine = **A**djacent / **H**ypotenuse
> **TOA** = **T**angent = **O**pposite / **A**djacent

### Formulas:

| Ratio | Formula | Abbreviation |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| **Sine** | sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse | SOH |
| **Cosine** | cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse | CAH |
| **Tangent** | tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent | TOA |
sin θ = O/H
cos θ = A/H
tan θ = O/A

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Finding a Side Using Sine (SOH)

**Problem:** In a right-angled triangle, θ = 30° and the hypotenuse is 10 cm. Find the opposite side (x).
A
|
|
x | \ 10 cm
|
|____
B 30° C

text

**Solution:**
sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
sin 30° = x / 10

text

Since sin 30° = 1/2:
1/2 = x / 10
x = 10 × 1/2
x = 5 cm

text

**Answer:** The opposite side is 5 cm.

---

### Example 2: Finding a Side Using Cosine (CAH)

**Problem:** In a right-angled triangle, θ = 60° and the hypotenuse is 20 cm. Find the adjacent side (x).
A
|
|
| \ 20 cm
|
|____
B 60° C
x

text

**Solution:**
cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
cos 60° = x / 20

text

Since cos 60° = 1/2:
1/2 = x / 20
x = 20 × 1/2
x = 10 cm

text

**Answer:** The adjacent side is 10 cm.

---

### Example 3: Finding a Side Using Tangent (TOA)

**Problem:** In a right-angled triangle, θ = 45° and the adjacent side is 8 cm. Find the opposite side (x).
A
|
|
x | \
|
|____
B 45° C
8 cm

text

**Solution:**
tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent
tan 45° = x / 8

text

Since tan 45° = 1:
1 = x / 8
x = 8 cm

text

**Answer:** The opposite side is 8 cm.

---

### Example 4: Finding an Angle Using Inverse Tangent

**Problem:** In a right-angled triangle, the opposite side is 6 cm and the adjacent side is 8 cm. Find the angle θ.
A
|
|
6 cm | \
|
|____
B θ C
8 cm

text

**Solution:**
tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent
tan θ = 6 / 8
tan θ = 0.75

text

To find θ, we use the **inverse tangent** (tan⁻¹):
θ = tan⁻¹(0.75)
θ ≈ 36.87°

text

**Answer:** The angle is approximately 36.87°.

---

### Example 5: Finding an Angle Using Inverse Sine

**Problem:** In a right-angled triangle, the opposite side is 5 cm and the hypotenuse is 13 cm. Find the angle θ.

**Solution:**
sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
sin θ = 5 / 13
sin θ ≈ 0.3846

text

To find θ, we use the **inverse sine** (sin⁻¹):
θ = sin⁻¹(0.3846)
θ ≈ 22.62°

text

**Answer:** The angle is approximately 22.62°.

---

### Example 6: Finding an Angle Using Inverse Cosine

**Problem:** In a right-angled triangle, the adjacent side is 12 cm and the hypotenuse is 13 cm. Find the angle θ.

**Solution:**
cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
cos θ = 12 / 13
cos θ ≈ 0.9231

text

To find θ, we use the **inverse cosine** (cos⁻¹):
θ = cos⁻¹(0.9231)
θ ≈ 22.62°

text

**Answer:** The angle is approximately 22.62°.

| 30° | 1/2 | √3/2 | 1/√3 |
| 45° | √2/2 | √2/2 | 1 |
| 60° | √3/2 | 1/2 | √3 |

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Heights and Distances

> A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall. The angle between the ladder and the ground is 60°. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?

**Solution:**
sin 60° = height / 10
height = 10 × sin 60°
height = 10 × √3/2
height = 5√3 ≈ 8.66 m

text

### Application 2: Finding Distances

> A person standing 50 m from a tree looks up at an angle of 30° to the top. How tall is the tree?

**Solution:**
tan 30° = height / 50
height = 50 × tan 30°
height = 50 × 1/√3
height ≈ 28.87 m

text

### Application 3: Navigation

> A ship sails 100 km on a bearing of 045°. How far east and how far north has it sailed?

**Solution:**
East = 100 × sin 45° = 100 × √2/2 ≈ 70.71 km
North = 100 × cos 45° = 100 × √2/2 ≈ 70.71 km

## 🎯 Practice Questions

### Easy Level

**Question 1:** In a right-angled triangle, sin θ = 3/5. Find cos θ.

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

Using the identity sin²θ + cos²θ = 1:
cos²θ = 1 - (3/5)² = 1 - 9/25 = 16/25
cos θ = 4/5
</details>

---

**Question 2:** In a right-angled triangle, the opposite side is 4 cm and the adjacent side is 3 cm. Find tan θ.

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent = 4/3
</details>

---

**Question 3:** Find the angle θ if sin θ = 0.5.

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

θ = sin⁻¹(0.5) = 30°
</details>

### Medium Level

**Question 4:** A ladder 15 m long leans against a wall at 70° to the ground. How high up the wall does it reach?

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

height = 15 × sin 70° ≈ 15 × 0.9397 ≈ 14.10 m
</details>

---

**Question 5:** From the top of a cliff, the angle of depression to a boat is 15°. If the cliff is 50 m high, how far is the boat from the base?

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

tan 15° = 50 / distance
distance = 50 / tan 15° ≈ 50 / 0.2679 ≈ 186.6 m
</details>
