# Division: Basic Operations

## 🎯 What is Division?

**Division** is the process of splitting a number into equal parts. It's the opposite of multiplication.

### Key Terms:
- **Dividend** = number being divided
- **Divisor** = number dividing
- **Quotient** = result of division
- **Remainder** = amount left over
12 ÷ 4 = 3

### Division Signs:
- ÷ (division sign)
- / (slash)
- — (fraction bar)

## 📊 Division Properties

| Property | Rule | Example |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Identity** | Dividing by 1 doesn't change | 5 ÷ 1 = 5 |
| **Zero** | Dividing by zero is undefined | 5 ÷ 0 = undefined |
| **Zero Dividend** | 0 ÷ 5 = 0 | 0 ÷ 5 = 0 |
| **Not Commutative** | Order matters | 12 ÷ 4 ≠ 4 ÷ 12 |

## 🔢 Division by 10, 100, 1000

### Rules:
- Divide by 10 → move decimal one place left
- Divide by 100 → move decimal two places left
- Divide by 1000 → move decimal three places left

### Example 1: Dividing by 10

**Problem:** 230 ÷ 10

**Solution:**
230 ÷ 10 = 23

## 🔢 Long Division

### Steps:
1. Divide the first digit
2. Multiply and subtract
3. Bring down the next digit
4. Repeat

**Step-by-Step:**
- 12 into 45 = 3 (write 3)
- 3 × 12 = 36
- 45 - 36 = 9
- Bring down 6 → 96
- 12 into 96 = 8 (write 8)
- 8 × 12 = 96
- 96 - 96 = 0
- Answer: 38

## 🔢 Dividing Integers

### Rule: Divide the absolute values and determine sign

| Problem | Sign | Solution |
|---------|------|----------|
| 15 ÷ 3 | Positive | 5 |
| 15 ÷ (-3) | Negative | -5 |
| -15 ÷ 3 | Negative | -5 |
| -15 ÷ (-3) | Positive | 5 |

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Sharing

> $124.00 shared equally among 8 people.
>
> **Each person gets:** $124.00 ÷ 8 = $15.50

### Application 2: Speed

> You travel 456 km in 12 hours.
>
> **Speed:** 456 ÷ 12 = 38 km/h

### Application 3: Price Per Unit

> 5 items cost $62.50.
>
> **Price per item:** $62.50 ÷ 5 = $12.50

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Forgetting to Bring Down Digits

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 456 ÷ 12 = 35 | 456 ÷ 12 = 38 |

---

### Mistake 2: Misplacing Decimal Point

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 4.56 ÷ 3 = 15.2 | 4.56 ÷ 3 = 1.52 |

---

### Mistake 3: Sign Errors

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| -15 ÷ -3 = -5 | -15 ÷ -3 = 5 |

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **Division** = splitting into equal parts
2. **Short division** = quick, one-digit divisor
3. **Long division** = step-by-step process
4. **Decimals:** move decimal point
5. **Signs:** like signs = positive, unlike signs = negative

### Division Vocabulary:
- **Dividend:** number being divided
- **Divisor:** number dividing
- **Quotient:** result of division
- **Remainder:** amount left over
