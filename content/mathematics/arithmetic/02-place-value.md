# Place Value and Total Value

## 🎯 What is Place Value?

**Place value** is the value of a digit based on its position in a number.

In the decimal system, each place has a value that is a power of 10.

## 🔑 Place Value, Face Value, and Total Value

### Place Value
The value of a digit based on its position.

### Face Value
The digit itself, regardless of position.

### Total Value
Place value × Face value

**Example:** In 4,582.37

| Digit | Face Value | Place | Place Value | Total Value |
|-------|------------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 4 | 4 | Thousands | 1,000 | 4,000 |
| 5 | 5 | Hundreds | 100 | 500 |
| 8 | 8 | Tens | 10 | 80 |
| 2 | 2 | Units | 1 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 | Tenths | 0.1 | 0.3 |
| 7 | 7 | Hundredths | 0.01 | 0.07 |

**Solution:**

## 📊 Comparing Numbers

### Rules:
1. Start from the leftmost digit
2. Compare digits in the same place value
3. If one digit is larger, that number is larger

### Symbols:

| Symbol | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| > | Greater than | 5 > 3 |
| < | Less than | 3 < 5 |
| = | Equal to | 5 = 5 |

---

### Example 5: Comparing Whole Numbers

**Problem:** Compare 3,456 and 3,789

**Solution:**
3,456 vs 3,789

Step 1: Thousands: 3 = 3
Step 2: Hundreds: 4 < 7
Therefore: 3,456 < 3,789

text

---

### Example 6: Comparing Decimals

**Problem:** Compare 4.56 and 4.59

**Solution:**
4.56 vs 4.59

Step 1: Units: 4 = 4
Step 2: Tenths: 5 = 5
Step 3: Hundredths: 6 < 9
Therefore: 4.56 < 4.59

text

## 📈 Ordering Numbers

### Ascending Order (Small to Large)
Numbers arranged from smallest to largest.

### Descending Order (Large to Small)
Numbers arranged from largest to smallest.

## 🔢 Rounding Off

### Rules for Rounding:
1. Look at the digit to the right of the place value you're rounding to
2. If it's 5 or more, round up
3. If it's 4 or less, round down

---

### Example 10: Round to Nearest Ten

**Problem:** Round 47 to the nearest ten

**Solution:**
47 → 50 (since 7 ≥ 5, round up)

text

---

### Example 11: Round to Nearest Hundred

**Problem:** Round 456 to the nearest hundred

**Solution:**
456 → 500 (since 5 ≥ 5, round up)

text

---

### Example 12: Round to Nearest Whole Number

**Problem:** Round 4.7 to the nearest whole number

4.7 → 5 (since 7 ≥ 5, round up)

text

### Example 13: Round to 2 Decimal Places

3.456 → 3.46 (since 6 ≥ 5, round up)

### Example 14: Round to Significant Figures

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Place Value Confusion

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| Place value of 7 in 3,758 is "7" | Place value of 7 in 3,758 is "hundreds" or "700" |

---

### Mistake 2: Rounding Incorrectly

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 4.45 → 4.4 (rounded to 1 d.p.) | 4.45 → 4.5 (since 5 rounds up) |

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **Place Value** = value based on position
2. **Face Value** = the digit itself
3. **Total Value** = place value × face value
4. **Expanded Form** = sum of total values
5. **Comparing** = start from leftmost digit
6. **Ordering** = ascending (small to large) or descending (large to small)
7. **Rounding** = look at the digit to the right
