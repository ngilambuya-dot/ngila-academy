# Indices / Exponents

## 🎯 What are Indices?

**Indices** (plural of index) tell us how many times to multiply a number by itself. Also called **exponents** or **powers**.

### Index Notation:
4³ = 4 × 4 × 4 = 64
↑ ↑
Base Index/Exponent

text

**Read as:** "4 to the power of 3" or "4 cubed"

---

## 📊 The Laws of Indices

### Law 1: Multiplication
When multiplying powers with the same base, **add** the indices.
aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ

text

**Example 1:**
2³ × 2² = 2³⁺² = 2⁵ = 32

text

**Example 2:**
x⁴ × x⁵ = x⁹

text

---

### Law 2: Division
When dividing powers with the same base, **subtract** the indices.
aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ

text

**Example 1:**
2⁵ ÷ 2³ = 2⁵⁻³ = 2² = 4

text

**Example 2:**
x⁷ ÷ x² = x⁵

text

---

### Law 3: Power of a Power
When raising a power to another power, **multiply** the indices.
(aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ

text

**Example:**
(2³)² = 2³×² = 2⁶ = 64

text

---

### Law 4: Zero Index
Any number raised to the power of zero equals **1**.
a⁰ = 1 (where a ≠ 0)

text

**Example:**
5⁰ = 1
x⁰ = 1

text

---

### Law 5: Negative Index
A negative index means the reciprocal of the positive power.
a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ

text

**Example 1:**
2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8

text

**Example 2:**
x⁻² = 1/x²

text

---

### Law 6: Fractional Index
Fractional indices represent roots.
a^(1/n) = ⁿ√a
a^(m/n) = ⁿ√(aᵐ)

text

**Example 1:**
25^(1/2) = √25 = 5

text

**Example 2:**
8^(2/3) = ∛(8²) = ∛64 = 4

text

## 🔢 Simplifying Expressions

### Example 1: Multiplication

**Problem:** Simplify 3² × 3⁴

**Solution:**
3² × 3⁴ = 3²⁺⁴ = 3⁶ = 729

text

---

### Example 2: Division

**Problem:** Simplify 7⁵ ÷ 7²

**Solution:**
7⁵ ÷ 7² = 7⁵⁻² = 7³ = 343

text

---

### Example 3: Power of a Power

**Problem:** Simplify (4²)³

**Solution:**
(4²)³ = 4²×³ = 4⁶ = 4096

text

---

### Example 4: Mixed Operations

**Problem:** Simplify (2³ × 2⁴) ÷ 2²

**Solution:**
2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷
2⁷ ÷ 2² = 2⁵ = 32

text

---

### Example 5: Negative Indices

**Problem:** Simplify 2⁻³

**Solution:**
2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8

text

---

### Example 6: Fractional Indices

**Problem:** Simplify 27^(2/3)

**Solution:**
27^(2/3) = (∛27)² = 3² = 9

text

---

## 📊 Proofs of Index Laws

### Proof of Multiplication Law:
2³ × 2² = (2×2×2) × (2×2) = 2⁵

text

### Proof of Zero Index:
2³ ÷ 2³ = 2³⁻³ = 2⁰ = 1

text

### Proof of Negative Index:
2⁻³ = 2⁰⁻³ = 2⁰ ÷ 2³ = 1/2³ = 1/8

text

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Multiplying when Adding

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 2³ × 2² = 4⁵ | 2³ × 2² = 2⁵ |

**Remember:** Keep the base the same, add the indices.

---

### Mistake 2: Wrong Order

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| (2³)² = 2⁵ | (2³)² = 2⁶ |

**Remember:** Multiply the indices.

| Law | Rule | Example |
