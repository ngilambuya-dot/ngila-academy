# Fractions

## 🎯 What is a Fraction?

A **fraction** represents a part of a whole. It shows how many equal parts we have out of the total number of parts.

### Parts of a Fraction:
3 ← Numerator (parts we have)
──
4 ← Denominator (total parts)

text

### Visual Representation:
Fraction: 3/4

[■■■□] = 3 parts out of 4 are shaded

text

---

## 📊 Types of Fractions

### 1. Proper Fractions
Numerator < Denominator (value is less than 1)
1/2, 3/4, 5/8

text

### 2. Improper Fractions
Numerator ≥ Denominator (value is 1 or more)
5/4, 7/3, 12/5

text

### 3. Mixed Numbers
A whole number and a proper fraction
1½, 2¾, 3⅓

text

### 4. Equivalent Fractions
Different fractions that represent the same value
1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8 = 8/16

text

---

## 🔄 Converting Between Fractions

### Example 1: Improper → Mixed Number

**Problem:** Convert 7/4 to a mixed number

**Solution:**
7 ÷ 4 = 1 remainder 3
7/4 = 1¾

text

**Steps:**
- Divide numerator by denominator
- Quotient becomes whole number
- Remainder becomes numerator
- Denominator stays the same

---

### Example 2: Mixed → Improper Fraction

**Problem:** Convert 2¾ to an improper fraction

**Solution:**
2¾ = (2 × 4 + 3)/4 = 11/4

text

**Steps:**
- Multiply whole number by denominator
- Add numerator
- Keep the same denominator

---

## 📊 Equivalent Fractions

### Rule: Multiply or divide both numerator and denominator by the same number

### Example 3: Find Equivalent Fractions

**Problem:** Find equivalent fractions of 1/2

**Solution:**
1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8 = 5/10 = 6/12

text

---

## 🔢 Simplifying Fractions

### Steps:
1. Find HCF of numerator and denominator
2. Divide both by HCF

### Example 4: Simplify 12/18

**Solution:**
HCF of 12 and 18 = 6
12 ÷ 6 = 2
18 ÷ 6 = 3
12/18 = 2/3

text

---

## ✍️ Adding Fractions

### Same Denominators:
Add numerators, keep denominator

### Different Denominators:
1. Find LCM of denominators (LCD)
2. Convert to equivalent fractions
3. Add numerators

### Example 5: Same Denominators

**Problem:** 3/8 + 2/8

**Solution:**
3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8

text

---

### Example 6: Different Denominators

**Problem:** 1/4 + 1/6

**Solution:**
LCM of 4 and 6 = 12
1/4 = 3/12
1/6 = 2/12
3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12

text

---

## ✍️ Subtracting Fractions

### Example 7: Same Denominators

**Problem:** 5/8 - 2/8

**Solution:**
5/8 - 2/8 = 3/8

text

---

### Example 8: Different Denominators

**Problem:** 3/4 - 1/6

**Solution:**
LCM of 4 and 6 = 12
3/4 = 9/12
1/6 = 2/12
9/12 - 2/12 = 7/12

text

---

## ✍️ Multiplying Fractions

### Rule: Multiply numerators, multiply denominators
a/b × c/d = (a × c)/(b × d)

text

### Example 9: Simple Multiplication

**Problem:** 2/3 × 4/5

**Solution:**
2/3 × 4/5 = 8/15

text

---

### Example 10: Mixed Numbers

**Problem:** 1½ × 2¾

**Solution:**
1½ = 3/2
2¾ = 11/4
3/2 × 11/4 = 33/8 = 4⅛

text

---

## ✍️ Dividing Fractions

### Rule: Keep, Change, Flip (KCF)
a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c

text

### Example 11: Simple Division

**Problem:** 2/3 ÷ 4/5

**Solution:**
2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6

text

---

### Example 12: Mixed Numbers

**Problem:** 1½ ÷ 2¾

**Solution:**
1½ = 3/2
2¾ = 11/4
3/2 ÷ 11/4 = 3/2 × 4/11 = 12/22 = 6/11

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Recipe

> A recipe needs 2/3 cup of sugar. You want to make half the recipe.
>
> **Sugar needed:** 2/3 × 1/2 = 1/3 cup

### Application 2: Pizza

> You ate 3/8 of a pizza. Your friend ate 2/8. How much did you eat together?
>
> **Total eaten:** 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8

### Application 3: Measuring

> You have 5/6 of a liter of water. You use 1/3 of a liter. How much is left?
>
> **Remaining:** 5/6 - 1/3 = 5/6 - 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 liter

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Adding Without Common Denominators

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 1/4 + 1/3 = 2/7 | 1/4 + 1/3 = 7/12 |

---

### Mistake 2: Dividing Fractions Incorrectly

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 8/15 | 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 5/6 |

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **Proper fraction:** numerator < denominator
2. **Improper fraction:** numerator ≥ denominator
3. **Mixed number:** whole + proper fraction
4. **Add/Subtract:** use common denominators
5. **Multiply:** multiply numerators and denominators
6. **Divide:** Keep, Change, Flip (KCF)
