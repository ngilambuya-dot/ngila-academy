# Powers, Squares, and Square Roots

## 🎯 What are Powers?

A **power** shows how many times to multiply a number by itself.

### Notation:
2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8
↑ ↑
Base Exponent

text

**Read as:** "2 to the power of 3" or "2 cubed"

---

## 🔢 Squares

A **square** is a number multiplied by itself.

### Notation:
3² = 3 × 3 = 9

## 📊 Square Roots

The **square root** of a number is the value that, when multiplied by itself, gives the original number.

### Notation:
√9 = 3 (since 3 × 3 = 9)

text

---

### Example 1: Perfect Square Roots

**Problem:** Find the square root of √25

**Solution:**
√25 = 5 (since 5 × 5 = 25)

text

---

### Example 2: Larger Perfect Square

**Problem:** Find √144

**Solution:**
√144 = 12 (since 12 × 12 = 144)

text

---

### Example 3: Estimating Square Roots

**Problem:** Estimate √20

**Solution:**
16 < 20 < 25
√16 < √20 < √25
4 < √20 < 5
√20 ≈ 4.47 (using calculator)

text

## 🔢 Cubes

A **cube** is a number multiplied by itself three times.

### Notation:
3³ = 3 × 3 × 3 = 27

## 📊 Cube Roots

The **cube root** of a number is the value that, when multiplied by itself three times, gives the original number.

### Notation:
∛27 = 3 (since 3 × 3 × 3 = 27)

text

---

### Example 4: Cube Roots

**Problem:** Find ∛64

**Solution:**
∛64 = 4 (since 4 × 4 × 4 = 64)

text

## 💡 Laws of Exponents

### Law 1: Multiplication
aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ

text

**Example:**
3² × 3³ = 3⁵ = 243

text

### Law 2: Division
aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ

text

**Example:**
3⁵ ÷ 3² = 3³ = 27

text

### Law 3: Power of a Power
(aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ

text

**Example:**
(3²)³ = 3⁶ = 729

text

### Law 4: Zero Exponent
a⁰ = 1 (where a ≠ 0)

text

### Law 5: Negative Exponent
a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ

text

### Law 6: Fractional Exponent
a^(1/n) = ⁿ√a

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Area

> A square has side length 5m. What is its area?
>
> **Area:** 5² = 25 m²

### Application 2: Volume

> A cube has side length 3cm. What is its volume?
>
> **Volume:** 3³ = 27 cm³

### Application 3: Distance

> The area of a square garden is 64 m². What is the side length?
>
> **Side:** √64 = 8m

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **Square:** number × itself (n²)
2. **Cube:** number × itself × itself (n³)
3. **Square root:** √n = value that squared = n
4. **Cube root:** ∛n = value that cubed = n
5. **Laws of exponents** help simplify calculations
