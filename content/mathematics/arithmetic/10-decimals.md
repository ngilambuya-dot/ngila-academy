# Decimals

## 🎯 What is a Decimal?

A **decimal** is a way of writing numbers that includes a decimal point. The decimal point separates the whole number part from the fractional part.

### Place Value Chart:
Thousands	Hundreds	Tens	Units	.	Tenths	Hundredths	Thousandths
1,000	100	10	1	.	0.1	0.01	0.001
text

### Example:
4,582.37

Digits: 4 5 8 2 . 3 7
Place: Thousands Hundreds Tens Units . Tenths Hundredths
Value: 4,000 + 500 + 80 + 2 . 0.3 + 0.07

## 🔄 Converting Decimals to Fractions

### Steps:
1. Write decimal as a fraction over 10, 100, 1000, etc.
2. Simplify if possible

### Example 1: Decimal → Fraction

**Problem:** Convert 0.5 to a fraction

**Solution:**
0.5 = 5/10 = 1/2

text

---

### Example 2: Two Decimal Places

**Problem:** Convert 0.75 to a fraction

**Solution:**
0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4

text

---

### Example 3: Three Decimal Places

**Problem:** Convert 0.125 to a fraction

**Solution:**
0.125 = 125/1000 = 1/8

text

---

## 🔄 Converting Fractions to Decimals

### Steps:
Divide numerator by denominator

### Example 4: Fraction → Decimal

**Problem:** Convert 1/4 to a decimal

**Solution:**
1 ÷ 4 = 0.25

text

---

### Example 5: Recurring Decimal

**Problem:** Convert 1/3 to a decimal

**Solution:**
1 ÷ 3 = 0.333... = 0.\overline{3}

text

## 🔄 Converting Decimals to Percentages

### Rule: Multiply by 100

### Example 6: Decimal → Percentage

**Problem:** Convert 0.75 to a percentage

**Solution:**
0.75 × 100 = 75%

text

---

## 🔄 Converting Percentages to Decimals

### Rule: Divide by 100

### Example 7: Percentage → Decimal

**Problem:** Convert 25% to a decimal

**Solution:**
25% ÷ 100 = 0.25

## ✍️ Multiplying Decimals

### Steps:
1. Multiply as whole numbers
2. Count total decimal places
3. Place decimal point from right

### Example 10: Multiplying Decimals

**Problem:** 3.4 × 2.5

**Solution:**
3.4 × 2.5 = 8.50

text

### Example 11: Dividing by Whole Number
