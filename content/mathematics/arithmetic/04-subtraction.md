# Subtraction: Basic Operations

## 🎯 What is Subtraction?

**Subtraction** is the process of taking away one number from another to find the difference.

### Key Terms:
- **Minuend** = number being subtracted from
- **Subtrahend** = number being subtracted
- **Difference** = the result
8 - 3 = 5
↑ ↑ ↑
Minuend Subtrahend Difference

text

### Minus Sign (-)
The - symbol means "minus" or "subtract."

---

## 📊 Subtraction Properties

| Property | Rule | Example |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Identity** | Subtracting zero doesn't change | 5 - 0 = 5 |
| **Inverse** | Subtracting a number is the opposite of adding | 5 - 5 = 0 |
| **Not Commutative** | Order matters | 5 - 3 ≠ 3 - 5 |

---

## 🔢 Subtraction Without Borrowing

### Steps:
1. Write numbers aligned by place value
2. Subtract each column starting from the right
3. Write the difference in the same place value

### Example 1: Simple Subtraction

**Problem:** Subtract 23 from 45

**Solution:**
45

23
────
22

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Units: 5 - 3 = 2
- Tens: 4 - 2 = 2
- Answer: 22

---

### Example 2: Three-Digit Subtraction

**Problem:** Subtract 234 from 567

**Solution:**
567

234
────
333

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Units: 7 - 4 = 3
- Tens: 6 - 3 = 3
- Hundreds: 5 - 2 = 3
- Answer: 333

## 🔢 Subtraction With Borrowing

### Steps:
1. Start from the rightmost column
2. If top digit is smaller, borrow from the next column
3. Decrease the next column by 1
4. Add 10 to the current column
5. Subtract

### Example 3: Borrowing

**Problem:** Subtract 37 from 52

**Solution:**
52

37
────
15

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Units: 2 < 7, so borrow from tens
- Tens: 5 becomes 4
- Units: 2 + 10 = 12
- 12 - 7 = 5
- Tens: 4 - 3 = 1
- Answer: 15

---

### Example 4: Multiple Borrowing

**Problem:** Subtract 456 from 723

**Solution:**
723

456
────
267

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Units: 3 < 6, borrow from tens
- Tens: 2 becomes 1
- Units: 3 + 10 = 13
- 13 - 6 = 7
- Tens: 1 < 5, borrow from hundreds
- Hundreds: 7 becomes 6
- Tens: 1 + 10 = 11
- 11 - 5 = 6
- Hundreds: 6 - 4 = 2
- Answer: 267

---

### Example 5: Borrowing Across Zeros

**Problem:** Subtract 456 from 700

**Solution:**
700

456
────
244

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Units: 0 < 6, borrow from tens
- Tens: 0 < 1, borrow from hundreds
- Hundreds: 7 becomes 6
- Tens: 0 + 10 = 10, then 10 - 1 = 9
- Units: 0 + 10 = 10
- 10 - 6 = 4
- Tens: 9 - 5 = 4
- Hundreds: 6 - 4 = 2
- Answer: 244

## 🔢 Subtracting Decimals

### Steps:
1. Align decimal points
2. Add zeros if needed
3. Subtract as normal
4. Place decimal point in same position

### Example 6: Subtracting Decimals

**Problem:** Subtract 2.34 from 4.56

**Solution:**
4.56

2.34
────
2.22

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Hundredths: 6 - 4 = 2
- Tenths: 5 - 3 = 2
- Units: 4 - 2 = 2
- Answer: 2.22

---

### Example 7: Borrowing with Decimals

**Problem:** Subtract 3.56 from 5.2

**Solution:**
5.20 (add zero)

3.56
────
1.64

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Hundredths: 0 < 6, borrow from tenths
- Tenths: 2 becomes 1
- Hundredths: 0 + 10 = 10
- 10 - 6 = 4
- Tenths: 1 < 5, borrow from units
- Units: 5 becomes 4
- Tenths: 1 + 10 = 11
- 11 - 5 = 6
- Units: 4 - 3 = 1
- Answer: 1.64

## 🔢 Subtracting Integers

### Rule: Add the opposite

| Problem | Convert to | Solution |
|---------|------------|----------|
| 5 - 3 | 5 + (-3) | 2 |
| 5 - (-3) | 5 + 3 | 8 |
| -5 - 3 | -5 + (-3) | -8 |
| -5 - (-3) | -5 + 3 | -2 |

### Example 8: Subtract Positive Integers

**Problem:** 12 - 23

**Solution:**
12 - 23 = -11

### Example 9: Subtract Negative Integers

**Solution:**

-9 - 5 = -14

text

### Example 11: Negative Minus Negative

-12 - (-5) = -12 + 5 = -7

## 🔢 Subtraction in Different Number Bases

### Example 12: Binary Subtraction

**Problem:** Subtract 11₂ from 100₂

**Solution:**
100₂

11₂
────
1₂

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- Units: 0 < 1, borrow from twos
- Twos: 0 < 1, borrow from fours
- Fours: 1 becomes 0
- Twos: 0 + 2 = 2, then 2 - 1 = 1
- Units: 0 + 2 = 2, 2 - 1 = 1
- Answer: 1₂

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Shopping

> You have $50 and buy items costing $12.50, $8.75, and $5.25.
>
> **Total spent:** $12.50 + $8.75 + $5.25 = $26.50
> **Change:** $50.00 - $26.50 = $23.50

### Application 2: Distance

> You travel 12.5 km, then 1.8 km, then 3.2 km. Total distance is 17.5 km.
>
> **Remaining distance:** 17.5 - 12.5 - 1.8 - 3.2 = 0 km

### Application 3: Temperature

> Temperature drops from 25°C to 18°C.
>
> **Decrease:** 25 - 18 = 7°C

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Forgetting to Borrow

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 52 - 37 = 15 | 52 - 37 = 15 |

**Remember:** Always borrow when the top digit is smaller.

---

### Mistake 2: Misaligning Decimal Points

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 5.2 - 3.56 = 1.64 | 5.20 - 3.56 = 1.64 |

---

### Mistake 3: Sign Errors

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| -5 - (-8) = -13 | -5 - (-8) = 3 |

---

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **Subtraction** = taking away one number from another
2. **Align** place values and decimal points
3. **Borrow** when top digit is smaller
4. **Signs:** subtract positive = add negative, subtract negative = add positive
5. **Check** your work by adding

### Subtraction Vocabulary:
- **Minuend:** number being subtracted from
- **Subtrahend:** number being subtracted
- **Difference:** result of subtraction
- **Borrow:** taking from the next column
