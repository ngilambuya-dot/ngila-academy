📊 Types of Word Problems
Money Problems
Example 1: Shopping
Problem: A customer buys 3 items costing $4.50, $2.75, and $1.25. If they pay with a $10 bill, what is their change?

Solution:

text
Total cost = $4.50 + $2.75 + $1.25 = $8.50
Change = $10.00 - $8.50 = $1.50
Example 2: Discount
Problem: A $200 phone is on sale at 25% discount. What is the sale price?

Solution:

text
Discount = 25% of $200 = $50
Sale price = $200 - $50 = $150
Age Problems
Example 3: Age Difference
Problem: John is 12 years old. His father is 3 times as old. How old is the father?

Solution:

text
Father's age = 3 × 12 = 36 years
Example 4: Combined Ages
Problem: The sum of John and his father's ages is 48. John is 12. How old is father?

Solution:

text
Father's age = 48 - 12 = 36 years
Measurement Problems
Example 5: Area
Problem: A rectangular garden is 5m by 4m. What is the area?

Solution:

text
Area = length × width
Area = 5 × 4 = 20 m²
Example 6: Perimeter
Problem: Same garden. What is the perimeter?

Solution:

text
Perimeter = 2(length + width)
Perimeter = 2(5 + 4) = 18 m
Speed, Distance, Time
Example 7: Distance
Problem: If you travel at 60 km/h for 3 hours, how far do you go?

Solution:

text
Distance = Speed × Time
Distance = 60 × 3 = 180 km
Example 8: Speed
Problem: You travel 240 km in 4 hours. What is the speed?

Solution:

text
Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Speed = 240 ÷ 4 = 60 km/h
Fractions and Percentages
Example 9: Fraction of a Quantity
Problem: 3/4 of students in a class of 24 are girls. How many are girls?

Solution:

text
Girls = 3/4 × 24 = 18
Example 10: Percentage of a Quantity
Problem: 65% of 200 students passed. How many passed?

Solution:

text
Passed = 65% × 200 = 0.65 × 200 = 130
Ratio Problems
Example 11: Sharing Ratio
Problem: Share $50 in ratio 3:2

Solution:

text
Total parts = 5
Value per part = $50 ÷ 5 = $10
First share = 3 × $10 = $30
Second share = 2 × $10 = $20
Example 12: Ratio Comparison
Problem: In a class, boys:girls = 3:2. If there are 15 boys, how many girls?

Solution:

text
3:2 = 15:g
3/2 = 15/g
3g = 30
g = 10 girls
Combined Operations
Example 13: Multi-Step Problem
Problem: John wants to buy a $120 item. He saves $15 per week for 6 weeks. How much more does he need?

Solution:

text
Saved = 6 × $15 = $90
Need = $120 - $90 = $30
Example 14: Real-Life Application
Problem: A phone plan costs $30/month. Additional data is $5/GB. If the bill is $65, how many GB were used?

Solution:

text
Additional cost = $65 - $30 = $35
GB used = $35 ÷ $5 = 7 GB
🌍 More Real-Life Applications
Application 1: Payroll
Employee earns $15/hour, works 40 hours/week. Weekly pay?

Pay: $15 × 40 = $600

Application 2: Cooking
Recipe serves 4 people. To serve 10 people, how much flour if recipe needs 2 cups?

Flour: 2 × (10/4) = 5 cups

Application 3: Budgeting
Monthly budget: Rent $800, Food $400, Transport $150, Other $250. Total?

Total: $800 + $400 + $150 + $250 = $1,600
