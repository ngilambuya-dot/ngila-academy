# BODMAS - Order of Operations

## 🎯 What is BODMAS?

**BODMAS** is a rule that tells us the order in which to solve mathematical expressions. It ensures everyone gets the same answer.

### BODMAS Meaning:

| Letter | Stands For | Meaning |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **B** | Brackets | Solve brackets first |
| **O** | Orders | Powers and square roots |
| **D** | Division | Divide (left to right) |
| **M** | Multiplication | Multiply (left to right) |
| **A** | Addition | Add (left to right) |
| **S** | Subtraction | Subtract (left to right) |

**Remember:** Division and Multiplication are equal priority (left to right).
**Remember:** Addition and Subtraction are equal priority (left to right).

## 📝 Examples

### Example 1: Simple BODMAS

**Problem:** 8 + 3 × 2

**Solution:**
8 + 3 × 2
= 8 + 6 (Multiplication first)
= 14 (Addition)

text

---

### Example 2: With Brackets

**Problem:** (8 + 3) × 2

**Solution:**
(8 + 3) × 2
= 11 × 2 (Brackets first)
= 22 (Multiplication)

text

---

### Example 3: With Division and Multiplication

**Problem:** 12 ÷ 3 × 2

**Solution:**
12 ÷ 3 × 2
= 4 × 2 (Division first, left to right)
= 8 (Multiplication)

text

---

### Example 4: With All Operations

**Problem:** 8 + 3 × (12 - 4) ÷ 2

**Solution:**
8 + 3 × (12 - 4) ÷ 2
= 8 + 3 × 8 ÷ 2 (Brackets first)
= 8 + 24 ÷ 2 (Multiplication)
= 8 + 12 (Division)
= 20 (Addition)

text

---

### Example 5: With Orders

**Problem:** 8 + 3² × 2

**Solution:**
8 + 3² × 2
= 8 + 9 × 2 (Orders first)
= 8 + 18 (Multiplication)
= 26 (Addition)

text

---

### Example 6: With Decimal

**Problem:** 8.5 + 3.2 × 2.5

**Solution:**
8.5 + 3.2 × 2.5
= 8.5 + 8.0 (Multiplication)
= 16.5 (Addition)

text

---

### Example 7: With Brackets and Decimals

**Problem:** (8.5 + 3.2) × 2.5

**Solution:**
(8.5 + 3.2) × 2.5
= 11.7 × 2.5 (Brackets)
= 29.25 (Multiplication)

text

## 🔢 BODMAS with Integers

### Example 8: Positive and Negative

**Problem:** -8 + 3 × (-2)

**Solution:**
-8 + 3 × (-2)
= -8 + (-6) (Multiplication)
= -14 (Addition)

text

---

### Example 9: With Brackets

**Problem:** (-8 + 3) × (-2)

**Solution:**
(-8 + 3) × (-2)
= (-5) × (-2) (Brackets)
= 10 (Multiplication)

text

---

### Example 10: Mixed Operations

**Problem:** 15 ÷ (-3) + 4 × 2

**Solution:**
15 ÷ (-3) + 4 × 2
= -5 + 8 (Division and Multiplication)
= 3 (Addition)

text

---

## 🔢 BODMAS with Fractions

### Example 11: Fraction Operations

**Problem:** 1/2 + 1/4 × 2

**Solution:**
1/2 + 1/4 × 2
= 1/2 + 2/4 (Multiplication)
= 1/2 + 1/2 (Simplify)
= 1 (Addition)

text

---

### Example 12: Brackets with Fractions

**Problem:** (1/2 + 1/4) × 2

**Solution:**
(1/2 + 1/4) × 2
= (3/4) × 2 (Brackets)
= 6/4 (Multiplication)
= 1.5 or 3/2 (Simplify)

text

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Adding Before Multiplying

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 8 + 3 × 2 = 22 | 8 + 3 × 2 = 14 |

---

### Mistake 2: Forgetting Brackets

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 8 + 3 × (12 - 4) ÷ 2 = 40 | 8 + 3 × (12 - 4) ÷ 2 = 20 |

---

### Mistake 3: Sign Errors

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| -8 + 3 × (-2) = -2 | -8 + 3 × (-2) = -14 |

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **BODMAS** tells the order of operations
2. **Brackets** first
3. **Orders** second
4. **Division and Multiplication** third (left to right)
5. **Addition and Subtraction** last (left to right)

### Mnemonics:
- **B**rackets
- **O**rders
- **D**ivision
- **M**ultiplication
- **A**ddition
- **S**ubtraction
