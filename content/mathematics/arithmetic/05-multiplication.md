# Multiplication: Basic Operations

## 🎯 What is Multiplication?

**Multiplication** is repeated addition of the same number.

### Key Terms:
- **Multiplicand** = number being multiplied
- **Multiplier** = number doing the multiplying
- **Product** = the result
3 × 4 = 12
↑ ↑ ↑
Multiplicand Multiplier Product

text

### Multiplication Signs:
- × (times)
- · (dot)
- () (parentheses)

---

## 📊 Multiplication Properties

| Property | Rule | Example |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Commutative** | Order doesn't matter | 3 × 4 = 4 × 3 |
| **Associative** | Grouping doesn't matter | (3 × 4) × 2 = 3 × (4 × 2) |
| **Identity** | Multiplying by 1 doesn't change | 5 × 1 = 5 |
| **Zero** | Multiplying by 0 equals 0 | 5 × 0 = 0 |
| **Distributive** | Multiplication over addition | 3 × (4 + 5) = 3×4 + 3×5 |

## 🔢 Multiplication by 10, 100, 1000

### Rules:
- Multiply by 10 → add one zero to the right
- Multiply by 100 → add two zeros to the right
- Multiply by 1000 → add three zeros to the right

### Example 1: Multiplying by 10

**Problem:** 23 × 10

**Solution:**
23 × 10 = 230

## 🔢 Multiplication of Decimals by Powers of 10

### Rules:
- Multiply by 10 → move decimal one place right
- Multiply by 100 → move decimal two places right
- Multiply by 1000 → move decimal three places right

### Example 4: Decimal × 10

**Solution:**

## 🔢 Long Multiplication

### Steps:
1. Multiply each digit of the multiplicand by each digit of the multiplier
2. Write partial products
3. Add partial products

### Example 6: Two-Digit Multiplication

**Problem:** 23 × 45

**Solution:**
23
× 45
────
115 (23 × 5)

920 (23 × 40)
────
1035

text

**Step-by-Step:**
- 23 × 5 = 115
- 23 × 40 = 920
- 115 + 920 = 1,035

## 🔢 Multiplying Decimals

### Steps:
1. Multiply as if they were whole numbers
2. Count total decimal places in both factors
3. Place decimal point that many places from right

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

>
> **Area:** 4.5 × 3.2 = 14.4 m²

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Forgetting to Add Partial Products

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 23 × 45 = 115 | 23 × 45 = 1,035 |

---

### Mistake 2: Misplacing Decimal Point

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| 2.34 × 1.2 = 28.08 | 2.34 × 1.2 = 2.808 |

---

### Mistake 3: Sign Errors

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| -5 × -3 = -15 | -5 × -3 = 15 |

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **Multiplication** = repeated addition
2. **Order** doesn't matter (commutative)
3. **Multiply by 10, 100, 1000** by adding zeros
4. **Long multiplication** uses partial products
5. **Decimals:** count total decimal places
6. **Signs:** like signs = positive, unlike signs = negative
