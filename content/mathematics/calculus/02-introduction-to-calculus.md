# Introduction to Calculus

## 🎯 What is Calculus?

**Calculus** is the branch of mathematics that studies **change** and **motion**. It was independently developed by Isaac Newton and Gottfried Leibniz in the 17th century.

### Key Concept:
> **Calculus helps us understand how things change and accumulate.**

---

## 📊 The Two Main Branches

### 1. Differential Calculus
> **Studies rates of change and slopes of curves.**

| Question | Example |
|----------|---------|
| How fast is a car accelerating? | Speed is changing |
| What is the slope of a curve at a point? | Instantaneous rate of change |
| How does temperature change over time? | Rate of cooling/heating |
| What is the marginal cost in business? | Cost of producing one more item |

### 2. Integral Calculus
> **Studies accumulation and areas under curves.**

| Question | Example |
|----------|---------|
| How far did a car travel? | Total distance from speed |
| What is the area under a curve? | Total accumulation |
| How much total growth occurred? | Sum of small changes |
| What is the total profit? | Sum of marginal profits |

### The Connection:
> **Differentiation and integration are inverse operations—they undo each other.**

## 📊 The Concept of a Limit

A **limit** is the value that a function approaches as the input approaches some value.

### Example:
As x approaches 2, what is the limit of (x² - 4)/(x - 2)?

text

**Problem:** At x = 2, the expression is 0/0 (undefined).

**Solution:** Use limits!
lim(x→2) (x² - 4)/(x - 2) = lim(x→2) (x+2)(x-2)/(x-2) = lim(x→2) (x+2) = 4

text

**Explanation:** Even though the function is undefined at x = 2, we can see what value it approaches as x gets closer and closer to 2.

## 📊 Historical Background

### Isaac Newton (1642-1727)
- Developed **fluxions** (his version of calculus)
- Used calculus to explain planetary motion
- Formulated the laws of motion and gravitation

### Gottfried Leibniz (1646-1716)
- Developed calculus independently
- Invented the modern **notation** we use today (dy/dx, ∫)
- First to use the term "calculus"

### The Calculus Wars
- Newton and Leibniz both claimed to have invented calculus first
- Modern historians credit both with independent discoveries
