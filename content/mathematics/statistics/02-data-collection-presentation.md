# Data Collection and Presentation

## 🎯 Methods of Data Collection

### 1. Surveys
> **Asking people questions to gather information.**

**Example:** A school survey to find students' favorite subjects.

### 2. Observations
> **Watching and recording behavior or events.**

**Example:** Counting the number of cars that pass a certain point.

### 3. Experiments
> **Conducting controlled tests to gather data.**

**Example:** Testing the effect of different fertilizers on plant growth.

### 4. Existing Data
> **Using data that has already been collected.**

**Example:** Using government census data.

---

## 📊 Frequency Distribution Tables

### Definition:
> **A table that shows how often each value occurs in a dataset.**

### Example: Student Test Scores

**Raw Data:** 65, 70, 75, 80, 85, 70, 65, 80, 75, 70, 90, 85, 75, 70, 80

**Frequency Table:**

| Score (x) | Tally | Frequency (f) |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| 65 | II | 2 |
| 70 | IIII | 4 |
| 75 | III | 3 |
| 80 | III | 3 |
| 85 | II | 2 |
| 90 | I | 1 |
| **Total** | | **15** |

---

### Grouped Frequency Distribution

**When to use:** When data has many different values.

**Example:** Student Ages (15-25)

| Age Group | Frequency |
|-----------|-----------|
| 15-17 | 5 |
| 18-20 | 12 |
| 21-23 | 8 |
| 24-26 | 5 |

## 📊 Pie Charts

### Definition:
> **A circular graph that shows parts of a whole.**

### Calculating Angles:
Angle = (Frequency / Total) × 360°

text

### Example: Favorite Subjects

| Subject | Frequency | Angle |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Math | 10 | (10/30)×360 = 120° |
| English | 8 | (8/30)×360 = 96° |
| Science | 7 | (7/30)×360 = 84° |
| History | 5 | (5/30)×360 = 60° |
| **Total** | **30** | **360°** |

### Difference from Bar Charts:
| Bar Chart | Histogram |
|-----------|-----------|
| Gaps between bars | No gaps between bars |
| Categories | Numerical intervals |

## 📊 Frequency Polygons

### Definition:
> **A line graph that shows the shape of the distribution.**

### Steps:
1. Find the midpoint of each class interval
2. Plot the midpoint against the frequency
3. Join the points with straight lines
