# Frequency Distribution Tables - Ungrouped Data

## 🎯 What is a Frequency Distribution?

A **frequency distribution** is a systematic arrangement of data showing how many times each value or category occurs. It summarizes large amounts of data into an organized table that is easy to interpret.

### Key Concept:
> **Frequency distribution organizes raw data into a meaningful and interpretable form.**

---

## 📊 Components of a Frequency Distribution

| Column | Description | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Score/Value** | The data values or categories | 5, 6, 7, 8 |
| **Tally** | Counting marks (||||) | |||| |
| **Frequency (f)** | Number of occurrences | 5 |
| **Percent (%)** | Percentage of total | 25% |
| **Cumulative Frequency (CF)** | Running total of frequencies | 15 |

## 📊 Calculating Percentages

### Formula:
Percent = (Frequency / Total Frequency) × 100%

text

**Example:**
For score 72: f = 3, N = 20
Percent = (3/20) × 100 = 15%

text

## 📊 Cumulative Frequency

### Definition:
> **Cumulative frequency is the running total of frequencies.**

### Types:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Less Than CF** | Running total from lowest to highest |
| **More Than CF** | Running total from highest to lowest |

### Example:

| Score | f | Less Than CF | More Than CF |
|-------|---|--------------|--------------|
| 64 | 1 | 1 | 20 |
| 66 | 2 | 3 | 19 |
| 68 | 1 | 4 | 17 |
| 69 | 1 | 5 | 16 |
| 70 | 2 | 7 | 15 |
| 72 | 3 | 10 | 13 |
| 73 | 1 | 11 | 10 |
| 74 | 1 | 12 | 9 |
| 75 | 1 | 13 | 8 |
| 76 | 2 | 15 | 7 |
| 78 | 1 | 16 | 5 |
| 80 | 1 | 17 | 4 |
| 81 | 1 | 18 | 3 |
| 82 | 1 | 19 | 2 |
| 83 | 1 | 20 | 1 |

## 📊 Percentile Rank

### Definition:
> **Percentile rank is the percentage of scores that fall at or below a particular score.**

### Formula:
Percentile Rank = (CF / N) × 100%

text

### Example:
Score 72 has CF = 10, N = 20
Percentile Rank = (10/20) × 100% = 50th percentile

text

**Interpretation:** A score of 72 is at the 50th percentile (better than 50% of learners).
