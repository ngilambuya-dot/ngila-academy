# Geometric Series (Sum to Infinity)

## 🎯 What is an Infinite Series?

An **infinite series** is the sum of all terms in an infinite sequence.

### Key Concept:
> **An infinite series can have a finite sum if the terms get smaller and smaller.**

---

## 📊 Convergence and Divergence

### Convergence
When the terms get smaller and approach zero, the sum approaches a finite value.
Example: 1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + 1/16 + ... = 2

text

### Divergence
When the terms do not approach zero, the sum goes to infinity.
Example: 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 16 + ... = ∞

text

---

## 📊 Sum to Infinity Formula

### Condition:
> **The sum to infinity exists only when |r| < 1.**

### Formula:
S∞ = a / (1 - r)

text

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Sum to Infinity

**Problem:** Find the sum to infinity: 16 + 8 + 4 + 2 + 1 + ...

**Solution:**
a = 16, r = 1/2
|r| = 0.5 < 1 → Converges!

S∞ = 16 / (1 - 1/2)
S∞ = 16 / (1/2)
S∞ = 32

### Example 3: Divergent Series

**Problem:** Check if the series converges: 10 + 20 + 40 + 80 + ...

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Pharmacy

> A patient takes 100mg of medication. The body removes 20% each hour.
>
> **Amount in body:** 100 + 80 + 64 + 51.2 + ...
> **S∞ = 100 / (1 - 0.8) = 100 / 0.2 = 500mg**

### Application 2: Geometry

> A ball bounces to 2/3 of its previous height.
>
> **Total distance traveled:** Infinite series.

### Application 3: Zeno's Paradox

> To cross a room, you must first go half way, then half of the remaining, etc.
>
> **Distance:** 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + ... = 1
