# Arithmetic Series

## 🎯 What is a Series?

A **series** is the sum of the terms of a sequence.

### Key Concept:
> **Sequence = List of numbers**
> **Series = Sum of the numbers**

### Example:
Sequence: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10
Series: 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 30

## 📊 Arithmetic Series

### Definition:
> The sum of an arithmetic sequence is called an **arithmetic series**.

### Formula:
Sₙ = n/2 × (a + l)

text
Where:
- Sₙ = sum of n terms
- n = number of terms
- a = first term
- l = last term

### Alternative Formula:
Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n-1)d]

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Finding the Sum

**Problem:** Find the sum of: 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10

**Solution:**
Method 1: Direct addition
S = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 30

Method 2: Using formula
a = 2, l = 10, n = 5
S₅ = 5/2 × (2 + 10)
S₅ = 2.5 × 12 = 30

text

---

### Example 2: Sum of First n Terms

**Problem:** Find the sum of the first 10 terms: 3, 6, 9, 12, ...

**Solution:**
a = 3, d = 3, n = 10
S₁₀ = 10/2 × [2(3) + (10-1)3]
S₁₀ = 5 × [6 + 27]
S₁₀ = 5 × 33 = 165

text

---

### Example 3: Finding Number of Terms

**Problem:** The sum of an arithmetic series is 120. The first term is 2, the last term is 22. How many terms?

**Solution:**
Sₙ = n/2 × (a + l)
120 = n/2 × (2 + 22)
120 = n/2 × 24
120 = 12n
n = 10

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Savings

> You save $5 on day 1, $10 on day 2, $15 on day 3, etc. How much have you saved after 20 days?

**Solution:**
a = 5, d = 5, n = 20
S₂₀ = 20/2 × [2(5) + (20-1)5]
S₂₀ = 10 × [10 + 95]
S₂₀ = 10 × 105 = $1050

text

---

### Application 2: Classroom Seating

> A theater has 10 rows. The first row has 12 seats, each row increases by 2 seats. Total seats?

**Solution:**
a = 12, d = 2, n = 10
S₁₀ = 10/2 × [2(12) + (10-1)2]
S₁₀ = 5 × [24 + 18]
S₁₀ = 5 × 42 = 210 seats
