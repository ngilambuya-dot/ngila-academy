# Simple Interest - Complete Guide

## 🎯 What is Simple Interest?

**Simple Interest** is interest calculated on the original principal only. It does not compound.

### Key Concept:
> **Simple Interest = Principal × Rate × Time**

### Why Does Simple Interest Matter?

1. **Savings accounts** - Banks pay interest on deposits
2. **Loans** - Borrowers pay interest to lenders
3. **Investments** - Money grows over time
4. **Business** - Understanding the cost of borrowing

---

## 📊 Key Terms in Simple Interest

| Term | Symbol | Definition | Example |
|------|--------|------------|---------|
| **Principal** | P | Original amount of money | KSh 10,000 |
| **Rate** | R | Interest rate per year | 10% |
| **Time** | T | Duration in years | 3 years |
| **Interest** | I | Amount earned or paid | KSh 3,000 |
| **Amount** | A | Total after interest | KSh 13,000 |

---

## 📊 Simple Interest Formulas

### The Basic Formula:
I = P × R × T

text

### Amount Formula:
A = P + I
A = P(1 + RT)

text

### Finding P, R, or T:
P = I / (R × T)
R = I / (P × T)
T = I / (P × R)

text

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Calculating Simple Interest

**Problem:** KSh 10,000 is invested at 10% simple interest per year for 3 years. Calculate:
a) The interest earned
b) The total amount

**Solution:**

**a) Interest:**
P = 10,000
R = 10% = 0.10
T = 3 years

I = P × R × T
I = 10,000 × 0.10 × 3
I = KSh 3,000

text

**b) Amount:**
A = P + I
A = 10,000 + 3,000 = KSh 13,000

text

---

### Example 2: Calculating Amount

**Problem:** KSh 20,000 is invested at 8% simple interest per year for 5 years. Find the amount.

**Solution:**
P = 20,000
R = 8% = 0.08
T = 5 years

A = P(1 + RT)
A = 20,000(1 + 0.08 × 5)
A = 20,000(1 + 0.40)
A = 20,000 × 1.40
A = KSh 28,000

text

---

### Example 3: Finding Principal

**Problem:** A sum of money invested at 12% simple interest per year for 4 years earns KSh 9,600. Find the principal.

**Solution:**
I = 9,600
R = 12% = 0.12
T = 4 years

P = I / (R × T)
P = 9,600 / (0.12 × 4)
P = 9,600 / 0.48
P = KSh 20,000

text

---

### Example 4: Finding Rate

**Problem:** KSh 50,000 earns KSh 15,000 in simple interest over 5 years. Find the rate.

**Solution:**
P = 50,000
I = 15,000
T = 5 years

R = I / (P × T)
R = 15,000 / (50,000 × 5)
R = 15,000 / 250,000
R = 0.06 = 6%

text

---

### Example 5: Finding Time

**Problem:** How long will it take for KSh 40,000 to earn KSh 12,000 at 10% simple interest per year?

**Solution:**
P = 40,000
I = 12,000
R = 10% = 0.10

T = I / (P × R)
T = 12,000 / (40,000 × 0.10)
T = 12,000 / 4,000
T = 3 years

text

---

### Example 6: Finding Time When Given Amount

**Problem:** How long will it take for KSh 30,000 to grow to KSh 42,000 at 10% simple interest per year?

**Solution:**
P = 30,000
A = 42,000
R = 10% = 0.10

I = A - P = 42,000 - 30,000 = 12,000

T = I / (P × R)
T = 12,000 / (30,000 × 0.10)
T = 12,000 / 3,000
T = 4 years

text

---

### Example 7: Different Time Periods

**Problem:** KSh 60,000 is invested at 12% simple interest per year for 9 months. Find the interest.

**Solution:**
P = 60,000
R = 12% = 0.12
T = 9 months = 9/12 = 0.75 years

I = P × R × T
I = 60,000 × 0.12 × 0.75
I = 60,000 × 0.09
I = KSh 5,400

text

---

### Example 8: Converting Time

**Problem:** KSh 25,000 is invested at 8% simple interest per year for 150 days. Find the interest.

**Solution:**
P = 25,000
R = 8% = 0.08
T = 150 days = 150/365 years

I = 25,000 × 0.08 × 150/365
I = 25,000 × 0.08 × 0.41096
I = KSh 821.92

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Savings Account

> You deposit KSh 100,000 in a bank at 6% simple interest per year. After 3 years, you will have:
> - Interest: 100,000 × 0.06 × 3 = KSh 18,000
> - Total: KSh 118,000

### Application 2: Loan

> You borrow KSh 200,000 at 10% simple interest per year for 2 years.
> - Interest: 200,000 × 0.10 × 2 = KSh 40,000
> - Total to repay: KSh 240,000

### Application 3: Business Investment

> A business invests KSh 500,000 at 8% simple interest per year for 5 years.
> - Total amount: 500,000(1 + 0.08 × 5) = KSh 700,000
