# Hire Purchase - Complete Guide

## 🎯 What is Hire Purchase?

**Hire Purchase (HP)** is a method of buying goods where the buyer pays an initial deposit and then makes regular instalment payments over a period of time. The buyer does not own the item until the final payment is made.

### Key Concept:
> **Hire Purchase = Deposit + Instalments (with interest)**

### Why Do People Use Hire Purchase?

1. **To afford expensive items** - Spread the cost over time
2. **To avoid saving a large amount** - Pay as you use
3. **To get the item immediately** - No need to wait until you have the full amount

### Important Note:
> **Hire Purchase is more expensive than paying cash!**

## 📊 Key Terms in Hire Purchase

| Term | Definition | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Cash Price** | The price if paid in full upfront | KSh 50,000 |
| **Deposit** | Initial payment (usually a percentage) | 10% = KSh 5,000 |
| **Balance** | Remaining amount after deposit | KSh 45,000 |
| **Instalments** | Regular payments (monthly, weekly, etc.) | KSh 5,000 per month |
| **Duration** | Time period to pay instalments | 10 months |
| **Total HP Price** | Deposit + Total instalments | KSh 55,000 |
| **Extra Cost** | HP Price - Cash Price | KSh 5,000 |

---

## 📊 Hire Purchase Calculations

### Formula:
Total HP Price = Deposit + Total Instalments
Total Instalments = Instalment Amount × Number of Instalments
Extra Cost = Total HP Price - Cash Price

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Basic Hire Purchase

**Problem:** A TV costs KSh 50,000 cash. The store offers it on hire purchase with a 10% deposit and 10 monthly instalments of KSh 5,000 each. Calculate:

**a)** The deposit amount
**b)** The total amount paid
**c)** The extra cost

**Solution:**

**a) Deposit:**
Deposit = 10% of 50,000 = 0.10 × 50,000 = KSh 5,000

text

**b) Total amount paid:**
Deposit: KSh 5,000
Instalments: 10 × 5,000 = KSh 50,000
Total HP Price = 5,000 + 50,000 = KSh 55,000

text

**c) Extra cost:**
HP Price: KSh 55,000
Cash Price: KSh 50,000
Extra Cost = 55,000 - 50,000 = KSh 5,000

text

### Example 2: Hire Purchase with Percentage Deposit

**Problem:** A laptop costs KSh 80,000 cash. The store offers:
- Deposit: 15% of cash price
- Balance paid in 12 equal monthly instalments
- Interest charged: 10% of the balance

Calculate:
**a)** The deposit amount
**b)** The balance after deposit
**c)** The interest charged
**d)** The monthly instalment
**e)** Total amount paid

**Solution:**

**a) Deposit:**
Deposit = 15% of 80,000 = 0.15 × 80,000 = KSh 12,000

text

**b) Balance:**
Balance = 80,000 - 12,000 = KSh 68,000

text

**c) Interest:**
Interest = 10% of 68,000 = 0.10 × 68,000 = KSh 6,800

text

**d) Monthly instalment:**
Total to pay = 68,000 + 6,800 = KSh 74,800
Monthly instalment = 74,800 ÷ 12 = KSh 6,233.33

text

**e) Total HP Price:**
Deposit: KSh 12,000
Total instalments: KSh 74,800
Total HP Price = 12,000 + 74,800 = KSh 86,800

text

### Example 3: Finding the Cash Price

**Problem:** A refrigerator is bought on hire purchase. The deposit was 20% of the cash price. The balance was paid in 18 monthly instalments of KSh 6,000 each. The HP price was KSh 120,000. Find the cash price.

**Solution:**
HP Price = KSh 120,000
Total instalments = 18 × 6,000 = KSh 108,000
Deposit = 120,000 - 108,000 = KSh 12,000

If deposit is 20% of cash price:
20% of CP = 12,000
CP = 12,000 ÷ 0.20 = KSh 60,000

Answer: Cash Price = KSh 60,000

### Example 4: Comparing Cash and HP

**Problem:** A car costs KSh 1,200,000 cash. On hire purchase:

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Deposit | 20% of cash price |
| Instalments | 24 months |
| Monthly payment | KSh 50,000 |

**a)** Calculate the deposit
**b)** Calculate the total HP price
**c)** How much more does the customer pay in hire purchase?

**Solution:**

**a) Deposit:**
Deposit = 20% of 1,200,000 = 0.20 × 1,200,000 = KSh 240,000

text

**b) Total HP Price:**
Instalments = 24 × 50,000 = KSh 1,200,000
HP Price = 240,000 + 1,200,000 = KSh 1,440,000

text

**c) Extra Cost:**
Extra = 1,440,000 - 1,200,000 = KSh 240,000

## 📊 Summary

### Key Formulas:

| Concept | Formula |
|---------|---------|

| HP Price | Deposit + Total Instalments |

### Key Terms:
- **Cash Price:** Full price paid upfront
- **Deposit:** Initial payment
- **Instalments:** Regular payments
- **HP Price:** Total amount paid
- **Extra Cost:** Additional amount paid
