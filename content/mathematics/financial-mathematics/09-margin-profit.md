# Margin Profit - Complete Guide

## 🎯 What is Margin Profit?

**Margin Profit** is the percentage of revenue that remains after accounting for costs. It shows how much profit a business makes from each shilling of sales.

### Key Concept:
> **Margin is the percentage of selling price that is profit.**

### Margin vs Markup:

| Concept | Definition | Formula |
|---------|------------|---------|
| **Markup** | Percentage of cost price added to get selling price | (SP - CP)/CP × 100% |
| **Margin** | Percentage of selling price that is profit | (SP - CP)/SP × 100% |

### Why Margin Matters:

1. **Measures profitability** - Tells how much profit per sale
2. **Pricing decisions** - Helps set appropriate prices
3. **Business performance** - Indicates efficiency
4. **Competitive analysis** - Compare with competitors

---

## 📊 Types of Profit Margin

### 1. Gross Profit Margin

Measures profit after deducting the cost of goods sold.
Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold
Gross Profit Margin = (Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100%

text

### 2. Net Profit Margin

Measures profit after deducting all expenses (including operating expenses, taxes, interest).
Net Profit = Revenue - All Expenses
Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100%

text

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Calculating Gross Profit Margin

**Problem:** A business sells goods worth KSh 500,000. The cost of goods sold is KSh 300,000. Calculate the gross profit margin.

**Solution:**
Revenue = KSh 500,000
Cost of Goods Sold = KSh 300,000
Gross Profit = 500,000 - 300,000 = KSh 200,000

Gross Profit Margin = (200,000 / 500,000) × 100%
Gross Profit Margin = 40%

text

**Answer:** The gross profit margin is 40%.

---

### Example 2: Calculating Net Profit Margin

**Problem:** A business has:
- Revenue: KSh 1,000,000
- Cost of Goods Sold: KSh 600,000
- Operating Expenses: KSh 200,000
- Tax: KSh 40,000

Calculate the net profit margin.

**Solution:**
Revenue = KSh 1,000,000
Total Expenses = 600,000 + 200,000 + 40,000 = KSh 840,000
Net Profit = 1,000,000 - 840,000 = KSh 160,000

Net Profit Margin = (160,000 / 1,000,000) × 100%
Net Profit Margin = 16%

text

---

### Example 3: Finding Selling Price Given Cost and Margin

**Problem:** A product costs KSh 400 to make. The business wants a 25% profit margin. What should be the selling price?

**Solution:**
Cost = KSh 400
Margin = 25% = 0.25

Margin = (SP - CP) / SP
0.25 = (SP - 400) / SP
0.25SP = SP - 400
400 = SP - 0.25SP
400 = 0.75SP
SP = 400 / 0.75
SP = KSh 533.33

Answer: Selling Price = KSh 533.33

text

---

### Example 4: Finding Cost Given Selling Price and Margin

**Problem:** An item is sold for KSh 1,200. The business makes a 30% margin. What is the cost price?

**Solution:**
SP = KSh 1,200
Margin = 30% = 0.30

Margin = (SP - CP) / SP
0.30 = (1,200 - CP) / 1,200
0.30 × 1,200 = 1,200 - CP
360 = 1,200 - CP
CP = 1,200 - 360
CP = KSh 840

Answer: Cost Price = KSh 840

text

### Example 5: Markup vs Margin Comparison

**Problem:** A business buys goods for KSh 200 and sells them for KSh 300.

**a)** Calculate the markup percentage
**b)** Calculate the margin percentage

**Solution:**

**a) Markup:**
Markup = (SP - CP) / CP × 100%
Markup = (300 - 200) / 200 × 100%
Markup = 100 / 200 × 100%
Markup = 50%

text

**b) Margin:**
Margin = (SP - CP) / SP × 100%
Margin = (300 - 200) / 300 × 100%
Margin = 100 / 300 × 100%
Margin = 33.33%

text

**Answer:** Markup is 50%, Margin is 33.33%.

### Example 6: Margin from Multiple Products

**Problem:** A business sells three products:

| Product | Cost Price | Selling Price |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| A | KSh 100 | KSh 150 |
| B | KSh 200 | KSh 300 |
| C | KSh 50 | KSh 80 |

Calculate the margin for each product.

**Solution:**

**Product A:**
Margin = (150 - 100) / 150 × 100%
Margin = 50 / 150 × 100%
Margin = 33.33%

text

**Product B:**
Margin = (300 - 200) / 300 × 100%
Margin = 100 / 300 × 100%
Margin = 33.33%

text

**Product C:**
Margin = (80 - 50) / 80 × 100%
Margin = 30 / 80 × 100%
Margin = 37.5%

text
