# Money Basics - From Cents to Shillings

## 🎯 What is Money?

**Money** is anything that is widely accepted as payment for goods and services. It is used to buy things, pay for services, and save for the future.

### Why Do We Need Money?

1. **To buy food** - We use money to buy groceries and meals
2. **To pay for services** - We pay for transport, electricity, water
3. **To save for the future** - We can keep money for emergencies
4. **To help others** - We can give money to those in need

## 🔄 Converting Between Cents and Shillings

### Rule:
To convert cents to shillings → divide by 100
To convert shillings to cents → multiply by 100

## 🛒 Shopping with Money

### Example 5: Adding Money

**Problem:** Mary buys a book for KSh 150, a pen for KSh 25, and a ruler for KSh 18. How much did she spend?

**Solution:**
Book: KSh 150
Pen: KSh 25
Ruler: KSh 18
Total: KSh 193

text

---

### Example 6: Buying Multiple Items

**Problem:** John went to the shop with KSh 500. He bought:
- 2 kg of sugar at KSh 120 per kg
- 1 litre of cooking oil at KSh 220
- 1 packet of tea leaves at KSh 80

How much did he spend and how much balance did he get?

**Solution:**
Step 1: Calculate cost of sugar
2 × 120 = KSh 240

Step 2: Add all costs
Sugar: KSh 240
Cooking oil: KSh 220
Tea leaves: KSh 80
Total spent: KSh 540

Step 3: Calculate balance
Amount given: KSh 500
Total spent: KSh 540
John needs KSh 40 more!

text

**Answer:** John spent KSh 540 and needs KSh 40 more.

---

### Example 7: Shopping List with Fractions

**Problem:** A customer bought:
- 3 packets of flour at KSh 45 each
- 1/2 kg of sugar at KSh 120 per kg
- 1 kg of rice at KSh 180
- 2 litres of milk at KSh 65 per litre

How much did the customer pay? If they gave KSh 500, what was their balance?

**Solution:**
Step 1: Cost of flour
3 × 45 = KSh 135

Step 2: Cost of sugar
1/2 kg of sugar = 0.5 × 120 = KSh 60

Step 3: Cost of rice
1 × 180 = KSh 180

Step 4: Cost of milk
2 × 65 = KSh 130

Step 5: Total cost
Flour: KSh 135
Sugar: KSh 60
Rice: KSh 180
Milk: KSh 130
Total: KSh 505

Step 6: Balance
Given: KSh 500
Spent: KSh 505
Balance: KSh -5 (needs KSh 5 more)

text
