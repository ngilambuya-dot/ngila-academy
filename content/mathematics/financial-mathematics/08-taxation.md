# Taxation (PAYE) - Complete Guide

## 🎯 What is Taxation?

**Taxation** is the process by which the government collects money from individuals and businesses to fund public services such as roads, schools, hospitals, and security.

### Key Concept:
> **Taxation is a mandatory contribution to the government to support public services and infrastructure.**

### Why Do We Pay Tax?

1. **To fund public services** - Education, healthcare, infrastructure
2. **To support the government** - Running the country
3. **To reduce inequality** - Redistribution of wealth
4. **To regulate the economy** - Control inflation and spending

---

## 📊 Key Terms in Taxation

| Term | Definition | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Gross Income** | Total earnings before deductions | KSh 100,000 |
| **Taxable Income** | Income subject to tax | KSh 80,000 |
| **PAYE** | Pay As You Earn - tax deducted from salary | KSh 20,000 |
| **Personal Relief** | Amount deducted from tax payable | KSh 2,400/month |
| **Net Income** | Income after all deductions | KSh 75,000 |
| **Tax Rate** | Percentage of income paid as tax | 30% |

---

## 📊 PAYE - Pay As You Earn

**PAYE** is a system where employers deduct income tax from employees' salaries before paying them. The tax is then remitted to the government.

### Formula:
Taxable Income = Gross Income - Non-Taxable Deductions
Tax Payable = (Taxable Income × Tax Rate) - Reliefs
Net Income = Gross Income - Total Deductions

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Calculating PAYE

**Problem:** A person earns a gross salary of KSh 80,000 per month. The tax rates are:

| Income Bracket | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| Up to KSh 24,000 | 10% |
| KSh 24,001 - KSh 48,000 | 15% |
| KSh 48,001 - KSh 80,000 | 20% |

Calculate the PAYE.

**Solution:**
Step 1: Calculate tax for each bracket

Bracket 1: 24,000 × 0.10 = KSh 2,400
Bracket 2: (48,000 - 24,000) = 24,000 × 0.15 = KSh 3,600
Bracket 3: (80,000 - 48,000) = 32,000 × 0.20 = KSh 6,400

Step 2: Total tax before relief
Total Tax = 2,400 + 3,600 + 6,400 = KSh 12,400

Step 3: Apply personal relief (if any)
If personal relief = KSh 2,400
Tax Payable = 12,400 - 2,400 = KSh 10,000

Answer: PAYE = KSh 10,000

### Example 2: Calculating Net Income with PAYE

**Problem:** A person earns a gross salary of KSh 120,000 per month. Use the following tax rates:

| Income Bracket | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| Up to KSh 24,000 | 10% |
| KSh 24,001 - KSh 48,000 | 15% |
| KSh 48,001 - KSh 72,000 | 20% |
| KSh 72,001 - KSh 96,000 | 25% |
| Above KSh 96,000 | 30% |

Personal relief = KSh 2,400 per month. Calculate the net income.

**Solution:**
Step 1: Calculate tax for each bracket

Bracket 1: 24,000 × 0.10 = KSh 2,400
Bracket 2: (48,000 - 24,000) = 24,000 × 0.15 = KSh 3,600
Bracket 3: (72,000 - 48,000) = 24,000 × 0.20 = KSh 4,800
Bracket 4: (96,000 - 72,000) = 24,000 × 0.25 = KSh 6,000
Bracket 5: (120,000 - 96,000) = 24,000 × 0.30 = KSh 7,200

Step 2: Total tax before relief
Total Tax = 2,400 + 3,600 + 4,800 + 6,000 + 7,200 = KSh 24,000

Step 3: Apply personal relief
Tax Payable = 24,000 - 2,400 = KSh 21,600

Step 4: Calculate net income
Net Income = Gross Income - Tax Payable
Net Income = 120,000 - 21,600 = KSh 98,400

Answer: Net Income = KSh 98,400

### Example 3: Calculating Net Income with NSSF and NHIF

**Problem:** A person earns a gross salary of KSh 90,000 per month. The following deductions apply:
- PAYE: KSh 14,000
- NSSF: KSh 500
- NHIF: KSh 1,200

Calculate the net income.

**Solution:**
Gross Income: KSh 90,000
Total Deductions = 14,000 + 500 + 1,200 = KSh 15,700
Net Income = 90,000 - 15,700 = KSh 74,300

Answer: Net Income = KSh 74,300

### Example 4: Finding Gross Income from Net Income

**Problem:** After all deductions, a person receives KSh 65,000. The total deductions were:
- PAYE: KSh 12,000
- NSSF: KSh 500
- NHIF: KSh 1,200

Find the gross income.

**Solution:**
Net Income = KSh 65,000
Total Deductions = 12,000 + 500 + 1,200 = KSh 13,700
Gross Income = Net Income + Total Deductions
Gross Income = 65,000 + 13,700 = KSh 78,700

Answer: Gross Income = KSh 78,700

## 📊 Summary

### Key Formulas:

| Concept | Formula |
|---------|---------|
| Taxable Income | Gross Income - Non-Taxable Deductions |
| PAYE | Taxable Income × Tax Rate - Reliefs |
| Net Income | Gross Income - Total Deductions |
| Gross Income | Net Income + Total Deductions |

### Key Terms:
- **Gross Income:** Total earnings before deductions
- **PAYE:** Tax deducted from salary
- **Reliefs:** Amounts deducted from tax payable
- **Net Income:** Income after all deductions
