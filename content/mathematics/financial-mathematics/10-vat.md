# VAT - Value Added Tax

## 🎯 What is VAT?

**VAT** (Value Added Tax) is a tax added to the price of goods and services at each stage of production and distribution. It is ultimately paid by the final consumer.

### Key Concept:
> **VAT is a consumption tax paid by the final consumer of goods and services.**

---

## 📊 Key Terms in VAT

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **VAT Rate** | Percentage charged on goods/services (e.g., 16%) |
| **Price Excluding VAT** | Price before VAT is added |
| **VAT Amount** | The tax amount added to the price |
| **Price Including VAT** | Total price including tax |

---

## 📊 VAT Formulas
VAT Amount = Price × VAT Rate
Price Including VAT = Price + VAT Amount
VAT Amount = Price Including VAT × (VAT Rate / (1 + VAT Rate))

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Calculating VAT

**Problem:** A TV costs KSh 50,000 excluding VAT. The VAT rate is 16%. Calculate:

**a)** The VAT amount
**b)** The total price including VAT

**Solution:**

**a) VAT Amount:**
VAT = 50,000 × 0.16 = KSh 8,000

text

**b) Total Price:**
Total = 50,000 + 8,000 = KSh 58,000

text

---

### Example 2: Finding Price Before VAT

**Problem:** The total price of an item is KSh 34,800 including VAT at 16%. Find the price before VAT.

**Solution:**
Total = Price × 1.16
34,800 = Price × 1.16
Price = 34,800 / 1.16 = KSh 30,000

text

---

### Example 3: Multiple Items with VAT

**Problem:** A customer buys the following items:

| Item | Price (Excl. VAT) |
|------|------------------|
| Item 1 | KSh 5,000 |
| Item 2 | KSh 8,000 |
| Item 3 | KSh 3,000 |

Calculate the total including VAT at 16%.

**Solution:**
Total before VAT = 5,000 + 8,000 + 3,000 = KSh 16,000
VAT Amount = 16,000 × 0.16 = KSh 2,560
Total including VAT = 16,000 + 2,560 = KSh 18,560

text

---

### Example 4: VAT on Discounted Items

**Problem:** A shop gives a 10% discount on an item priced at KSh 20,000 (excluding VAT). Calculate the amount to pay including VAT at 16%.

**Solution:**
Price before discount: KSh 20,000
Discount = 10% of 20,000 = KSh 2,000
Price after discount = 20,000 - 2,000 = KSh 18,000
VAT = 18,000 × 0.16 = KSh 2,880
Total = 18,000 + 2,880 = KSh 20,880

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Shopping

> When you buy goods, VAT is included in the final price.

### Application 2: Business

> Businesses must register for VAT and charge it on their sales.

### Application 3: Invoicing

> Invoices must show the price excluding VAT, VAT amount, and total.
