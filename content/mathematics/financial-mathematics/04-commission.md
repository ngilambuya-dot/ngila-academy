# Commission - Complete Guide

## 🎯 What is Commission?

**Commission** is a payment made to an employee (typically a salesperson) based on the value of goods or services they sell. It is a form of incentive to encourage more sales.

### Key Concept:
> **Commission = Percentage of total sales value**

### Why Do Businesses Use Commission?

1. **To motivate employees** - Higher sales = higher earnings
2. **To reward performance** - The best salespeople earn the most
3. **To reduce fixed costs** - Businesses pay based on results
4. **To align interests** - The business and employee both want more sales

---

## 📊 Types of Commission

### 1. Commission Without Salary

The employee only earns commission. If they make no sales, they earn nothing.

**Formula:**
Commission = Sales Amount × Commission Rate

text

### Example 1: Commission Without Salary

**Problem:** A salesperson earns 10% commission on all sales. If they sell goods worth KSh 500,000 in a month, how much commission do they earn?

**Solution:**
Commission Rate = 10% = 0.10
Sales Amount = KSh 500,000

Commission = 500,000 × 0.10
Commission = KSh 50,000

Answer: The salesperson earns KSh 50,000

---

### Example 2: Commission Without Salary

**Problem:** A car salesperson earns 5% commission on each car sold. If they sell a car worth KSh 2,500,000, how much commission do they earn?

**Solution:**
Commission = 2,500,000 × 0.05
Commission = KSh 125,000

Answer: The salesperson earns KSh 125,000

text

---

### 2. Commission With Salary

The employee receives a basic salary plus commission on sales.

**Formula:**
Total Earnings = Salary + Commission

text

### Example 3: Commission With Salary

**Problem:** A salesperson has a basic salary of KSh 20,000 per month and earns 5% commission on all sales. If they sell goods worth KSh 400,000, what is their total earnings?

**Solution:**
Salary = KSh 20,000
Commission = 400,000 × 0.05 = KSh 20,000

Total Earnings = 20,000 + 20,000 = KSh 40,000

Answer: The salesperson earns KSh 40,000

**Solution:**
Salary = KSh 30,000
Commission = 250,000 × 0.08 = KSh 20,000

Total Earnings = 30,000 + 20,000 = KSh 50,000

Answer: The agent earns KSh 50,000

### 3. Commission Above a Given Sales Target

The employee earns commission only on sales above a certain target.

**Formula:**
Commission = (Sales Amount - Target) × Commission Rate
Total Earnings = Salary + Commission

text

### Example 5: Commission Above Target

**Problem:** A salesperson has a salary of KSh 25,000. They earn 10% commission on all sales above KSh 200,000. If they sell goods worth KSh 350,000, what is their total earnings?

**Solution:**
Salary = KSh 25,000
Target = KSh 200,000
Sales Amount = KSh 350,000
Sales above target = 350,000 - 200,000 = 150,000

Commission = 150,000 × 0.10 = KSh 15,000

Total Earnings = 25,000 + 15,000 = KSh 40,000

Answer: The salesperson earns KSh 40,000

text

---

### Example 6: Commission Above Target With Different Rates

**Problem:** A salesperson earns:
- Salary: KSh 15,000
- 5% commission on sales between KSh 100,000 and KSh 200,000
- 8% commission on sales above KSh 200,000

If they sell goods worth KSh 300,000, what is their total earnings?

**Solution:**
Salary = KSh 15,000

Tier 1: Sales between 100,000 and 200,000
= (200,000 - 100,000) × 0.05
= 100,000 × 0.05 = KSh 5,000

Tier 2: Sales above 200,000
= (300,000 - 200,000) × 0.08
= 100,000 × 0.08 = KSh 8,000

Commission = 5,000 + 8,000 = KSh 13,000

Total Earnings = 15,000 + 13,000 = KSh 28,000

Answer: The salesperson earns KSh 28,000

## 📊 Commission Rate Tables

### Example 7: Using Commission Rate Tables

**Problem:** A company pays commissions based on the following table:

| Sales Amount | Commission Rate |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Up to KSh 100,000 | 0% |
| KSh 100,001 to KSh 300,000 | 5% |
| Above KSh 300,000 | 8% |

If a salesperson sells goods worth KSh 500,000, calculate their commission.

**Solution:**
Tier 1: Up to 100,000 → 0% commission
Tier 2: 100,001 to 300,000
= (300,000 - 100,000) × 0.05
= 200,000 × 0.05 = KSh 10,000

Tier 3: Above 300,000
= (500,000 - 300,000) × 0.08
= 200,000 × 0.08 = KSh 16,000

Commission = 10,000 + 16,000 = KSh 26,000

Answer: Commission = KSh 26,000

## 📊 Summary

### Key Formulas:

| Type | Formula |
|------|---------|
| Commission Without Salary | Sales × Rate |
| Commission With Salary | Salary + Commission |
| Commission Above Target | Sales - Target × Rate |

### Key Terms:
- **Commission Rate:** The percentage earned on sales
- **Target:** Minimum sales required before commission is earned
- **Tiered Commission:** Different rates for different sales levels
