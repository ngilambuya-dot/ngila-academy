# Exchange Rates - Foreign Currency

## 🎯 What is an Exchange Rate?

An **exchange rate** is the value of one currency expressed in terms of another currency. It tells you how much of one currency you can get for another.

### Key Concept:
> **Exchange rates allow us to convert money from one currency to another.**

### Real-Life Example:
If you travel to the USA, you need to exchange your Kenyan Shillings (KES) to US Dollars (USD) to pay for goods and services.

## 🔄 Converting Currencies

### Rule:
To convert from local to foreign → multiply by the exchange rate
To convert from foreign to local → divide by the exchange rate

text

### Example 1: Converting KES to USD

**Problem:** 1 USD = 150 KES. How many US Dollars can you get for KSh 30,000?

**Solution:**
Amount in KES: 30,000
Exchange rate: 1 USD = 150 KES

To convert KES to USD: divide by rate
USD = 30,000 ÷ 150 = 200

Answer: KSh 30,000 = 200 USD

text

---

### Example 2: Converting USD to KES

**Problem:** 1 USD = 150 KES. How many Kenyan Shillings is 50 USD?

**Solution:**
Amount in USD: 50
Exchange rate: 1 USD = 150 KES

To convert USD to KES: multiply by rate
KES = 50 × 150 = 7,500

Answer: 50 USD = KSh 7,500

text

---

### Example 3: Converting EUR to KES

**Problem:** 1 EUR = 160 KES. How much is 75 EUR in KES?

**Solution:**
75 × 160 = 12,000
75 EUR = KSh 12,000

text

---

### Example 4: Converting GBP to KES

**Problem:** 1 GBP = 190 KES. How much is 45 GBP in KES?

**Solution:**
45 × 190 = 8,550
45 GBP = KSh 8,550

## 📊 Exchange Rate Tables

### Example 5: Using a Forex Bureau Rate

**Problem:** The bank offers the following rates:

| Currency | Buying Rate | Selling Rate |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| USD | 148 KES | 152 KES |
| EUR | 155 KES | 160 KES |
| GBP | 185 KES | 190 KES |

**a)** A tourist has KSh 100,000 and wants to buy USD. How many USD will they get?

**Solution:**
To buy foreign currency, use the SELLING rate
100,000 ÷ 152 = 657.89 USD

text

**b)** A tourist returns with 500 USD and wants to exchange to KES. How much will they get?

**Solution:**
To sell foreign currency, use the BUYING rate
500 × 148 = 74,000 KES

text

**c)** A tourist wants to buy 200 EUR. How much KES do they need?

**Solution:**
To buy foreign currency, use the SELLING rate
200 × 160 = 32,000 KES

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Travel

**Problem:** A family is traveling to the UK. They need 1,500 GBP for their trip. If the exchange rate is 1 GBP = 190 KES, how much KES do they need?

**Solution:**
1,500 × 190 = 285,000 KES

text

### Application 2: Business

**Problem:** A business imports goods worth 10,000 USD from the USA. The exchange rate is 1 USD = 150 KES. How much does the business need in KES?

**Solution:**
10,000 × 150 = 1,500,000 KES

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:

1. **Exchange rate** = value of one currency in terms of another
2. **To convert KES to foreign** → divide by rate
3. **To convert foreign to KES** → multiply by rate
4. **Buying rate** = rate used to buy foreign currency from you
5. **Selling rate** = rate used to sell foreign currency to you

### Important Formulas:
Foreign Currency = Amount in KES ÷ Exchange Rate
Amount in KES = Foreign Currency × Exchange Rate
