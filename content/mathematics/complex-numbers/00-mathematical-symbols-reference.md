# Mathematical Symbols Reference

## 📊 Basic Symbols

| Symbol | Name | Read As | Example | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|---------|---------|
| = | Equals sign | "equals" | 2 + 3 = 5 | Equal to |
| ≠ | Not equal to | "not equal to" | 5 ≠ 3 | Not equal |
| ≈ | Approximately equal | "approximately equal to" | π ≈ 3.14 | Approximately |
| < | Less than | "less than" | 3 < 5 | Less than |
| > | Greater than | "greater than" | 5 > 3 | Greater than |
| ≤ | Less than or equal | "less than or equal to" | x ≤ 5 | At most 5 |
| ≥ | Greater than or equal | "greater than or equal to" | x ≥ 5 | At least 5 |
| ∈ | Element of | "is an element of" | x ∈ ℝ | x is a real number |
| ∉ | Not an element of | "is not an element of" | i ∉ ℝ | i is not real |
| ⊂ | Subset of | "is a subset of" | ℕ ⊂ ℤ | Natural numbers subset of integers |
| ∞ | Infinity | "infinity" | x → ∞ | Tends to infinity |
