# Introduction to Inequalities

## 🎯 What is an Inequality?

An **inequality** is a mathematical statement that compares two expressions using inequality symbols. Unlike equations, inequalities show that one side is **greater than**, **less than**, or **not equal to** the other.

### The Inequality Symbols:

| Symbol | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| `<` | Less than | `x < 5` (x is less than 5) |
| `>` | Greater than | `x > 5` (x is greater than 5) |
| `≤` | Less than or equal to | `x ≤ 5` (x is at most 5) |
| `≥` | Greater than or equal to | `x ≥ 5` (x is at least 5) |
| `≠` | Not equal to | `x ≠ 5` (x is not equal to 5) |

---

## 📚 Key Differences: Equations vs Inequalities

| Equations | Inequalities |
|-----------|--------------|
| `x + 3 = 7` | `x + 3 > 7` |
| Has **one** solution | Has **many** solutions |
| `x = 4` only | `x > 4` (any number greater than 4) |
| Graph: one point | Graph: a range of values |

## 🔑 Graphing Inequalities on a Number Line

### Rules for Graphing:

| Inequality | Graph Symbol | Direction |
|------------|--------------|-----------|
| `<` or `>` | Open circle ○ | Shade away from the number |
| `≤` or `≥` | Closed circle ● | Shade away from the number |

## ✍️ Solving Linear Inequalities

### Rule: Same as equations, BUT...

When you **multiply or divide by a negative number**, **FLIP the inequality sign**!

**Example:**
-2x < 6
-2x ÷ (-2) > 6 ÷ (-2) ← FLIP THE SIGN!
x > -3

text

**Problem:** Solve `x + 5 > 12`

**Solution:**

x > 7

### Example 4: Division by Negative (SIGN FLIP!)

**Problem:** Solve `-4x ≤ 20`

### Example 5: Multi-Step Inequality

**Problem:** Solve `3x - 7 > 14`

**Solution:**

**Problem:** Solve `5x + 2 < 2x + 14`

5x + 2 < 2x + 14
5x + 2 - 2x < 2x + 14 - 2x

3x + 2 - 2 < 14 - 2

x < 4

## 🔗 Compound Inequalities

A **compound inequality** combines two inequalities with "and" or "or".

### "AND" Inequalities (Intersection)

**Example:** Solve `2 < x < 7`

This means: `x > 2` AND `x < 7`

**Solution:** All numbers between 2 and 7

**Graph:**
○───●───○
2 4 7

text

---

### "OR" Inequalities (Union)

**Example:** Solve `x < 2` OR `x > 7`

**Solution:** Numbers less than 2 OR greater than 7

**Graph:**
◄───○ ○───►
2 7

text

---

### Example 7: Compound "AND"

**Problem:** Solve `-3 ≤ 2x + 1 < 5`

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** Split into two inequalities:
-3 ≤ 2x + 1 AND 2x + 1 < 5

text

**Step 2:** Solve each:
-3 ≤ 2x + 1 2x + 1 < 5
-3 - 1 ≤ 2x 2x < 5 - 1
-4 ≤ 2x 2x < 4
-2 ≤ x x < 2

text

**Step 3:** Combine:
-2 ≤ x < 2

text

**Graph:**
●───○
-2 2

text

---

### Example 8: Compound "OR"

**Problem:** Solve `x - 3 < -1` OR `x + 2 > 6`

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** Solve each:
x - 3 < -1 x + 2 > 6
x < -1 + 3 x > 6 - 2
x < 2 x > 4

text

**Step 2:** Combine:
x < 2 OR x > 4

text

**Graph:**
◄───○ ○───►
2 4

text

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Budget

**Problem:** You have $50 to spend on school supplies. Each notebook costs $3 and each pen costs $2. Write an inequality for the number of items you can buy.

**Solution:**
Let `n` = notebooks, `p` = pens
3n + 2p ≤ 50

text

---

### Application 2: Speed Limit

**Problem:** The speed limit is 65 mph. Write an inequality for the safe speed.

**Solution:**
s ≤ 65

text

---

### Application 3: Temperature

**Problem:** Water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C. Write an inequality for liquid water temperature.

**Solution:**
0 < t < 100

text

---

### Application 4: Minimum Wage

**Problem:** The minimum wage is $15 per hour. Write an inequality for earnings.

**Solution:**
w ≥ 15

text

---

### Application 5: Phone Plan

**Problem:** A phone plan costs $30 per month plus $0.10 per minute. The budget is $50. How many minutes can you use?

**Solution:**
30 + 0.10m ≤ 50
0.10m ≤ 20
m ≤ 200

text

**Answer:** At most 200 minutes

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Forgetting to Flip the Sign

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| `-3x < 6` → `x < -2` | `-3x < 6` → `x > -2` |

**Remember:** Flip the sign when multiplying/dividing by a negative!

---

### Mistake 2: Confusing "Less Than" with "Less Than or Equal To"

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| `x ≤ 5` graph with open circle | `x ≤ 5` graph with closed circle |

**Remember:** ≤ and ≥ use CLOSED circles

---

### Mistake 3: Incorrect "OR" vs "AND"

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| "x < 2 AND x > 5" | "x < 2 OR x > 5" |

**Remember:** "AND" means overlap, "OR" means either

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points:
1. **Inequalities** compare expressions using `<`, `>`, `≤`, `≥`
2. **Graphing** uses open circles for < > and closed circles for ≤ ≥
3. **Solving** is the same as equations, but FLIP THE SIGN when multiplying/dividing by a negative!
4. **Compound inequalities** use "and" (intersection) or "or" (union)
