# Introduction to Algebra

## 🎯 What is Algebra?

Algebra is like a **secret code** that uses letters and symbols to represent numbers. It's the language of mathematics that allows us to solve problems where some numbers are unknown.

### The Basic Idea

Imagine you have a secret number in your pocket. Instead of revealing it, you might say:

> **"I have a number. If I add 3 to it, I get 10."**

In algebra, we write this as:
x + 3 = 10

text

Where **x** represents your secret number!

## 🔑 Key Vocabulary

| Term | Symbol | Definition | Example |
|------|--------|------------|---------|
| **Variable** | `x`, `y`, `z` | A letter that represents an unknown number | `x` in `2x + 3` |
| **Coefficient** | Number | A number multiplied by a variable | `3` in `3x` |
| **Constant** | Number | A fixed number without a variable | `5` in `3x + 5` |
| **Expression** | `2x + 3` | A combination of variables, numbers, and operations | `2x + 3` |
| **Equation** | `2x + 3 = 7` | Two expressions with an equals sign | `2x + 3 = 7` |
| **Term** | `3x` | A single part of an expression | `3x`, `-5`, `2y²` |

## 💡 Understanding Variables

Variables can represent:
- **Unknown values** we want to find
- **Quantities that change** (like temperature)
- **General patterns** (formulas)

### Example: Representing Real Situations

| Situation | Algebraic Expression |
|-----------|---------------------|
| "A number plus 5" | `x + 5` |
| "Twice a number" | `2x` |
| "A number minus 3" | `x - 3` |
| "A number divided by 4" | `x ÷ 4` or `x/4` |
| "5 more than twice a number" | `2x + 5` |
| "3 less than a number" | `x - 3` |

## ✍️ Writing Algebraic Expressions

### Rule: Read carefully and translate step by step

**Step 1:** Identify the unknown → assign a variable
**Step 2:** Identify operations (+, -, ×, ÷)
**Step 3:** Write in the correct order

### Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Simple Expression

**Problem:** Write an expression for "a number increased by 7"

**Solution:**
- Let the number be `x`
- "Increased by 7" means add 7
- Expression: `x + 7`

---

#### Example 2: Multiple Operations

**Problem:** Write an expression for "twice a number, plus 5"

**Solution:**
- Let the number be `x`
- Twice a number = `2x`
- Plus 5 = `+ 5`
- Expression: `2x + 5`

---

#### Example 3: Subtraction

**Problem:** Write an expression for "6 less than a number"

**Solution:**
- Let the number be `x`
- "Less than" means subtract from
- 6 less than x = `x - 6`
- **Note:** NOT `6 - x` (that's a common mistake!)

---

#### Example 4: Division

**Problem:** Write an expression for "a number divided by 3"

**Solution:**
- Let the number be `x`
- Divided by 3 = `x ÷ 3` or `x/3`
- Expression: `x/3`

---

#### Example 5: Mixed Operations

**Problem:** Write an expression for "3 less than 4 times a number"

**Solution:**
- Let the number be `x`
- 4 times a number = `4x`
- 3 less than = `- 3`
- Expression: `4x - 3`

---

#### Example 6: Real-Life Problem

**Problem:** A taxi charges a fixed fee of $3 plus $2 per mile. Write an expression for the total cost.

**Solution:**
- Let `m` = number of miles
- $2 per mile = `2m`
- Fixed fee = `+ 3`
- Expression: `2m + 3`

## 🔢 Evaluating Expressions

**Evaluating** means finding the value of an expression by substituting a number for the variable.

### Steps:
1. Replace the variable with the number
2. Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS)

---

### Worked Examples

#### Example 7: Basic Substitution

**Problem:** Evaluate `3x - 2` when `x = 4`

**Solution:**
3x - 2
= 3(4) - 2 ← Replace x with 4
= 12 - 2 ← Multiply first
= 10 ← Subtract

text

**Answer:** 10

---

#### Example 8: With Multiplication

**Problem:** Evaluate `2x + 5` when `x = 3`

**Solution:**
2x + 5
= 2(3) + 5 ← Replace x with 3
= 6 + 5 ← Multiply
= 11 ← Add

text

**Answer:** 11

---

#### Example 9: With Exponents

**Problem:** Evaluate `x² + 3x - 1` when `x = 2`

**Solution:**
x² + 3x - 1
= (2)² + 3(2) - 1 ← Replace x with 2
= 4 + 6 - 1 ← Exponents, then multiplication
= 9 ← Add and subtract from left to right

text

**Answer:** 9

---

#### Example 10: With Fractions

**Problem:** Evaluate `(x + 5)/2` when `x = 7`

**Solution:**
(x + 5)/2
= (7 + 5)/2 ← Replace x with 7
= 12/2 ← Add in numerator
= 6 ← Divide

text

**Answer:** 6

---

#### Example 11: Multiple Variables

**Problem:** Evaluate `3a + 2b - 4` when `a = 5` and `b = 3`

**Solution:**
3a + 2b - 4
= 3(5) + 2(3) - 4 ← Replace variables
= 15 + 6 - 4 ← Multiply
= 17 ← Add and subtract

text

**Answer:** 17

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### 1. Shopping

> You buy `x` items at $15 each and pay a $5 delivery fee.
> **Expression:** `15x + 5`

### 2. Temperature Conversion

> Convert Celsius `C` to Fahrenheit `F`
> **Formula:** `F = (9/5)C + 32`

### 3. Salary Calculation

> You earn $20 per hour plus a $100 weekly bonus.
> **Expression:** `20h + 100` where `h` = hours worked

### 4. Distance, Speed, Time

> Distance = Speed × Time
> **Formula:** `d = st`

### 5. Compound Interest

> A = P(1 + r)ᵗ where:
> - A = Final amount
> - P = Principal
> - r = Interest rate
> - t = Time

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: "Less Than"

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| "5 less than x" written as `5 - x` | "5 less than x" is `x - 5` |

**Remember:** "Less than" means subtract FROM the number.

---

### Mistake 2: Order of Operations

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| `2 + 3x` when `x = 4` = `2 + 3 = 5, 5×4 = 20` | `2 + 3(4) = 2 + 12 = 14` |

**Remember:** Always do multiplication BEFORE addition.

---

### Mistake 3: Missing Parentheses

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| `3x²` when `x = 2` = `3 × 2² = 3 × 4 = 12` | `3(2)² = 3 × 4 = 12` |

**Remember:** The exponent applies to the variable, not the coefficient.

---

### Mistake 4: Confusing Coefficients

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| `3x` when `x = 2` = `32` | `3x` = `3 × 2 = 6` |

**Remember:** `3x` means `3 × x`, not "thirty something."

## 📊 Summary

### Key Points

1. **Variables** are letters that represent unknown numbers
2. **Expressions** combine variables, numbers, and operations
3. **Coefficients** are numbers multiplying variables
4. **Constants** are numbers without variables
5. **Evaluating** means substituting values for variables
6. Always follow **Order of Operations** (PEMDAS)

### The Process
Word Problem → Identify Unknown → Write Expression → Evaluate (if needed)
