# Solving Linear Equations by Substitution

## 🎯 What is a System of Linear Equations?

A **system of linear equations** is a set of two or more linear equations with the same variables. The solution is the point where all equations intersect (the values that satisfy ALL equations).

### Example of a System:
Equation 1: x + y = 10
Equation 2: x - y = 4

text

The solution is `x = 7, y = 3` because:
- 7 + 3 = 10 ✅
- 7 - 3 = 4 ✅

---

## 📚 When to Use Substitution Method

Use substitution when:
- One variable is already isolated (e.g., `y = 2x + 3`)
- One equation can be easily solved for one variable
- The coefficient of a variable is 1 or -1

---

## 🔑 The Substitution Method Steps

### Step-by-Step Process:

**Step 1:** Solve one equation for one variable
**Step 2:** Substitute that expression into the other equation
**Step 3:** Solve for the remaining variable
**Step 4:** Substitute back to find the other variable
**Step 5:** Check your solution in both equations

---

## ✍️ Worked Examples

### Example 1: Simple Substitution

**Problem:** Solve the system:
y = 2x + 3
x + y = 9

text

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** One equation is already solved for y: `y = 2x + 3`

**Step 2:** Substitute `2x + 3` for `y` in the second equation:
x + (2x + 3) = 9

text

**Step 3:** Solve for x:
3x + 3 = 9
3x = 6
x = 2

text

**Step 4:** Substitute x = 2 into `y = 2x + 3`:
y = 2(2) + 3
y = 4 + 3
y = 7

text

**Step 5:** Check:
Equation 1: 7 = 2(2) + 3 = 7 ✅
Equation 2: 2 + 7 = 9 ✅

text

**Answer:** `x = 2, y = 7`

---

### Example 2: Substitution with Isolation

**Problem:** Solve the system:
2x + y = 7
3x - 2y = 7

text

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** Solve the first equation for y:
2x + y = 7
y = 7 - 2x

text

**Step 2:** Substitute `7 - 2x` for `y` in the second equation:
3x - 2(7 - 2x) = 7

text

**Step 3:** Solve for x:
3x - 14 + 4x = 7
7x - 14 = 7
7x = 21
x = 3

text

**Step 4:** Substitute x = 3 into `y = 7 - 2x`:
y = 7 - 2(3)
y = 7 - 6
y = 1

text

**Step 5:** Check:
Equation 1: 2(3) + 1 = 7 ✅
Equation 2: 3(3) - 2(1) = 9 - 2 = 7 ✅

text

**Answer:** `x = 3, y = 1`

---

### Example 3: Substitution with Fractions

**Problem:** Solve the system:
x + 2y = 8
3x - y = 3

text

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** Solve the first equation for x:
x + 2y = 8
x = 8 - 2y

text

**Step 2:** Substitute `8 - 2y` for `x` in the second equation:
3(8 - 2y) - y = 3

text

**Step 3:** Solve for y:
24 - 6y - y = 3
24 - 7y = 3
-7y = -21
y = 3

text

**Step 4:** Substitute y = 3 into `x = 8 - 2y`:
x = 8 - 2(3)
x = 8 - 6
x = 2

text

**Step 5:** Check:
Equation 1: 2 + 2(3) = 8 ✅
Equation 2: 3(2) - 3 = 6 - 3 = 3 ✅

text

**Answer:** `x = 2, y = 3`

---

### Example 4: Substitution with Decimals

**Problem:** Solve the system:
y = 0.5x + 3
2x - y = 1

text

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** One equation is already solved for y: `y = 0.5x + 3`

**Step 2:** Substitute `0.5x + 3` for `y` in the second equation:
2x - (0.5x + 3) = 1

text

**Step 3:** Solve for x:
2x - 0.5x - 3 = 1
1.5x - 3 = 1
1.5x = 4
x = 4/1.5
x = 8/3

text

**Step 4:** Substitute x = 8/3 into `y = 0.5x + 3`:
y = 0.5(8/3) + 3
y = 4/3 + 3
y = 4/3 + 9/3
y = 13/3

text

**Answer:** `x = 8/3, y = 13/3`

---

### Example 5: Substitution with No Solution

**Problem:** Solve the system:
y = 2x + 3
y = 2x - 1

text

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** Both equations are solved for y

**Step 2:** Set them equal to each other:
2x + 3 = 2x - 1

text

**Step 3:** Solve:
3 = -1

text
This is FALSE! 

**Answer:** No solution (parallel lines, never intersect)

---

### Example 6: Substitution with Infinite Solutions

**Problem:** Solve the system:
y = 3x + 2
6x - 2y = -4

text

**Solution:**

**Step 1:** First equation is solved for y: `y = 3x + 2`

**Step 2:** Substitute `3x + 2` for `y` in the second equation:
6x - 2(3x + 2) = -4

text

**Step 3:** Solve:
6x - 6x - 4 = -4
-4 = -4

text
This is TRUE for all values of x!

**Answer:** Infinite solutions (same line)

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

### Application 1: Shopping Problem

**Problem:** You buy apples and oranges. Apples cost $2 each, oranges cost $3 each. You buy 8 fruits total and spend $22. How many of each did you buy?

**Solution:**
Let `a` = number of apples, `o` = number of oranges
a + o = 8 (total fruits)
2a + 3o = 22 (total cost)

text

**Step 1:** Solve first equation for a:
a = 8 - o

text

**Step 2:** Substitute:
2(8 - o) + 3o = 22
16 - 2o + 3o = 22
16 + o = 22
o = 6

text

**Step 3:** Find a:
a = 8 - 6 = 2

text

**Answer:** 2 apples, 6 oranges

---

### Application 2: Age Problem

**Problem:** A father is 3 times as old as his son. In 10 years, he will be 2 times as old. Find their ages.

**Solution:**
Let `f` = father's age, `s` = son's age
f = 3s (currently father is 3 times son)
f + 10 = 2(s + 10) (in 10 years, father is 2 times son)

text

**Step 1:** First equation is already solved: `f = 3s`

**Step 2:** Substitute:
3s + 10 = 2(s + 10)
3s + 10 = 2s + 20
s = 10

text

**Step 3:** Find f:
f = 3(10) = 30

text

**Answer:** Father is 30, son is 10

---

### Application 3: Mixture Problem

**Problem:** A chemist needs 20L of a 15% acid solution. She has a 10% solution and a 30% solution. How much of each should she mix?

**Solution:**
Let `x` = amount of 10% solution, `y` = amount of 30% solution
x + y = 20 (total volume)
0.10x + 0.30y = 0.15(20) (acid amount)

text

**Step 1:** Solve first equation for x:
x = 20 - y

text

**Step 2:** Substitute:
0.10(20 - y) + 0.30y = 3
2 - 0.10y + 0.30y = 3
2 + 0.20y = 3
0.20y = 1
y = 5

text

**Step 3:** Find x:
x = 20 - 5 = 15

text

**Answer:** 15L of 10% solution, 5L of 30% solution

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Sign Errors When Substituting

| ❌ WRONG | ✅ CORRECT |
|----------|-----------|
| `3x - (2y + 5) = 3x - 2y + 5` | `3x - (2y + 5) = 3x - 2y - 5` |

**Remember:** Subtract EVERY term in the parentheses!

---

### Mistake 2: Not Checking Both Equations

Always check your solution in BOTH original equations.

---

### Mistake 3: Not Simplifying Fractions

Always simplify your final answer.

---

## 📊 Summary

### Steps for Substitution:
1. Solve one equation for a variable
2. Substitute that expression into the other equation
3. Solve for the remaining variable
4. Substitute back to find the other variable
5. Check in BOTH equations

### Types of Solutions:
- **One solution** → Lines intersect at one point
- **No solution** → Lines are parallel
- **Infinite solutions** → Lines are the same
